What's a shower? You don't know it exists.

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 1 June 2025 Update: 1 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

The foot bath is an old relaxing technique! The foot bath, by definition, is the process of immersion, massage and nursing of the feet. This treatment has a long history in China, and many believe it can promote whole-body blood circulation and improve health.

When taking a bath, people usually use warm water and herbs and oils to enhance their efficacy. It helps not only to ease fatigue, but also to ease stress and relax.

The beauty of a shower.

There's a lot of good in the shower. It promotes blood circulation, improves sleep quality, relieves foot fatigue and pain. In addition, foot baths help to detoxify and promote health.

How to take a bath.

The method of bathing is quite simple. First of all, prepare a bowl of warm water with appropriate herbs or fine oil. Then he dipped his feet into water and enjoyed a quiet period of 15 to 30 minutes. Finally, a simple foot massage can take place to ease further.

Mind your business.

When taking a bath, be careful not to overheat the water so as not to burn. In addition, in the case of certain special cases, it is recommended that this be done under professional guidance.
