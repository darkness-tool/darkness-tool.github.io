Will the tug of the river break its hand? In the past, the terrorist movement has become a real tragedy.

Author: Big Man Date of issue: 8 September 2026 Update: 8 September 2026 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Don't underestimate the seemingly simple work of tug of the river! In the eyes of many, the tugs are simply a fun contest to hold tight the ropes and the crowds backwards, which is often seen in school sports or in corporate formations. However, behind this joyful event, there is a very high moment of tension and the risk of physical harm. On the Internet, people often ask, "Do you really pull your hand out?" The answer is: if the method is wrong, it can happen.

From the point of view of sports medicine, it is important to bear a huge burden on the various joints and muscles of the body during the tug of the river. According to the statistics and medical studies of the International Federation, the most frequent acute and chronic injuries in the region include muscle pulling, joint spraining, and even wrist-tunnel syndromes with more than 50% of the professional contestants. Most of these injuries are due to a long period of intense resistance and misperceptions.

Why did you hang your wrist? Deadly error habit.

Many people, in order to "grow their milk" when they pull up the river, are used to placing rough line around their wrists, arms and even their waists, thereby increasing friction to prevent hand slips. This is an extremely dangerous and deadly act! There have been a number of real cases in the past, when both sides are working at the same time, and the tight ropes are tightening at the same time, and their power is like an invisible blade, cutting their skins and crushing their joint bones on the spot, and cutting their arteries and muscles directly.

In addition to tearing and fractured wrists in daily or in-kid competitions due to infrequent entanglements, there have also been massive and tragic events in history that shocked societies. In 1997, for example, during the events in Taipei, the crowds of contestants were overstretched, resulting in a large-scale sideline that could not withstand pressure at the moment. The powerful reactionary force and the opening of the wires were the scene in which many of the contestants were re-inflicted, and even the tragic loss of limbs on the spot of many of their left arms, which became a historic pain that the Taiwanns were unable to carry on.

It's common to see chronic and acute injuries.

In addition to extreme amputations and hangings, more people face chronic and cumulative injuries. As both hands are required to maintain a high degree of static contraction for long periods of time during the tuning of the river, the nerve and blood vessels in the hand will continue to be pressured. After many years of training, many of the top tugs often suffer from numbing, incapacitating, and even from high pain resistance. It is only when the disease has increased that the nervous system has been severely damaged.

In addition, the waist and lower limbs are also a disaster area. The vertebrae and knee joints suffered a disproportionate shear due to the need to lean back and bear the weight of the team. Without enough warmth or the incompetence of the core muscles, it's very easy to create a waist in the moment of resistance. Quite a bit of vertebrate trauma, laceration or dislocated shoulder.

How can we enjoy the river safely?

The Pao River itself is an excellent movement that nurtures the team and can effectively avoid tragic events if it has the right security rules. First, it is absolutely forbidden to wrap the rope around the wrist, arm or any part of the body, and to hold it solely by hand; second, before entering the competition, it is necessary to check the material and quality of the rope, and to do sufficient hand, waist and lower limbs to warm up. Stay in the right position, be strong, have fun and be safe!
