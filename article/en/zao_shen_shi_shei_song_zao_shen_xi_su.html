Who is the god of the stoves? Why did the farmer send the god of the stoves years ago? It's a great secret to open up a thousand years of customs!

By Mina Date of issue: 24 January 2026 Update: 24 January 2026 Reading time: about 5 minutes

Do you have a tradition of delivering the god? Every time we arrive on Monday 23rd or 24th, families are busy preparing candy, pastries, and even rubbing on the mouths of a cooker, a day known as "The Year" or "The Festival of the Festival". Who is the god? Why do you have to send him to heaven a few years ago? The Chinese wisdom and faith, hidden behind this custom for thousands of years, allow us to explore it closely!

The god of the stove, also known as the master of the stove, the cook of the stove and the king of the kitchen, is one of the closest people in China's traditional faith. He is not the Emperor above, but the "owner of the house" in every kitchen. From time immemorial, when humans invented fire, the stove became the centre of food, and the god of the stove was in charge of the fire, the supervision of human consumption and the behaviour of the family.

The origin and identity of the god of the stove.

According to popular legends, Toto god is an inspector of the Jade Emperor and even says he is his brother or close friend. The mission of the god of the stoves is very special: he lived by his stoves all year long, watching in silence the good words and deeds of this family. All of them are not able to escape the eyes of the gods, except whether they are good to their parents, whether they are honest to others, and whether they are good or not.

The earliest rituals date back to the time of Qinhan, when people began to sacrifice the gods. In the north and south of Wei, the god of the stoves has come up with a specific name and image. After Song Dynasty, the rituals became more popular, and the idols of the Emperor were placed in the kitchens of his family, usually a powerful old man, accompanied by wives, image and affection.

Why did you say "The God of the Fire" a year ago?

Every year, on the twenty-third or twenty-four (mostly twenty-three in the north and twenty-four in the south), the god of the stove must return to heaven and report to the Emperor of Jade for what the family has done over the past year. The report determines whether it will be a blessing or a disaster in the coming year, so the day is called a "go-go" or "go-go." People want to talk good and bad in heaven, and that's why there's a saying that says good and bad.

A lot of sweet foods such as melons, malt sugars, tunics, pastry, pastries, etc. have been prepared in order to make a good word with the god. There is even the tradition of "drinking intoxicated" with wine, or the use of sugar on the mouth of the god of the stove, which means, "Sweet, sweet mouth, good words." This is a reflection of China's desire to trade small supplies for God-protected heart.

The ceremonial ceremony for the delivery of the stoves was very nuanced: first, the old statue of the stoves was removed, and the pony horses were burned (into the sky), then the stoves were cleaned, the offerings were put on the table and the fragrances were observed. After the prayer, the whole family bowed and prayed for "good things in heaven and good things in the world." The whole process was filled with dignity and joy, marking the end of the year and the coming of the new year.

North-South disparities and modern changes

It is worth mentioning that the date of the god of the stove is different from that of the north. The North is mostly on the 23rd and the South is on the 24th. In some places, on New Year's Eve's Eve, there'll be a new statue of the stove, and it will welcome the god back from heaven and continue to supervise the family.

With the change of time, old earth stoves are being replaced by gas stoves, electric magnets, and rituals are being diluted in cities. However, in many villages and traditional families, this tradition remains a key cultural memory linking the past and the present. It is not only a sacrifice, it is a way to remind families of morality and good deeds.

At the heart of his faith is the Chinese idea of "food for heaven" and the belief in the cause and effect of good and evil. Although small, I am in charge of the most basic of human consumption and family management. Through the stoves, people express their prayers for the coming year, and remind themselves of the times when they need to behave.

This little troupe God has carried the warmth and wisdom of Chinese culture for a thousand years. In the next few years, we may wish to make a simple offering at home, so that the tradition can continue in the modern world. Peace be upon the house and happiness for the next year.
