Why do you want to taste anything? The real reason why the cat and the tycoon become a "ghost" is revealed!

By Mina Date of issue: 26 September 2026 Update: 26 September 2026 Reading time: About 4 minutes

As long as you're at home with your potato chips or your lunches, the cats that were sleeping will wake up in a moment! This amazing thing happens in almost every cat-raising family. Whether you have freshly cooked fish in your bowl, fried chicken, or a salad that appears to have nothing to do with it, your normally cold, proud master will appear in front of you with precision and death to stare at your food with his eager and determined eyes, and even try to share a cup with his hairy little paws. Why is that?

Many newcomers often wonder why the masters continue to show unparalleled enthusiasm for humans’ foods, even though cat food is well balanced and even contains their favorite meat. In fact, it is not simply because they are hungry, but because they hide deep animal instincts, curiosity about the outside world, and the unique physiological structure of cats.

Reason one: Strong curiosity and desire to explore in the genes.

Cats are natural hunters who are highly sensitive to any wind and grass around the environment. Forebears who live in the wild, in order to ensure that they have access to sufficient variety of nutrients, explore any strange scent or novelty. When you bring out a hot, fragranced plate of food, the complex elements of the air immediately touch cat's sharp sense of smell and stimulate their exploration instincts in their bones, wondering what the new prey is.

In addition to curiosity, cats' pursuit of the smell is far beyond our imagination. Human foods are usually cooked, boiled, fried, fried and so on, which spreads a lot of oil and protein. For cats whose sense of smell is many times as sensitive as humans, these scents are an unparalleled sensory party that attracts them to want to get closer and closer.

Reason two: Imitation and social studies

You probably don't know. Cats really like the wind. In their eyes, the scavenger is like a huge, weird-looking cat mother or community leader. When they see you enjoying something, the brain comes to think, "Well, it's safe and delicious to be eaten so well by the boss. This imitation, which is based on observation and trust, will create a strong desire to follow them.

At the same time, it is a form of distraction. Sometimes cats are not really obsessed with the food itself, but they find that when they show interest in it, the scavengers turn their head and even provide interaction. The positive return of this "food can get attention if you get close to it" will make them a popular habit every time they eat.

Reason three: Unique cat food makes them want to change their taste.

Although cat food is impeccable in school, anyone who eats the same brand, the same flavor dry day after day will find it boring. Humans not only have a large variety of meals, but also a variety of attractive temperatures and varicious qualities. For cats that yearn for freshness, the fragrance on the table is like a secret and limited snack, which certainly makes them taste three feet.

But even though cats always act like they're pathetic, they don't eat and they don't stop. The spices that are common in many human foods, such as onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes and excess salt and grease, are severe toxins or burdens for the cat ' s body, which may lead to acute poisoning or chronic chronic diseases of long duration.

How can you be smart about the "strength" of the King?

In order to protect the health of their masters and at the same time satisfy their curiosity, we can take some smart responses. For example, if cats are separated while eating themselves, or if they are prepared for their own health, they are fed, frozen, and when cats come in, their attention is diverted to their own snacks. In this way, both cats feel "I'm eating good food" and avoid the health risks of human food.

In conclusion, the cat's passion for the food in your plate is driven by a natural charisma, a keen sense of smell, and the trust and imitation in you. The next time they look at you with their innocent eyes, remember to give her a loving hug and a healthy cat snack, instead of giving them a good share of danger!

It is hoped that this in-depth analysis will help you to learn more about the care of your master! If you have one next to you every time you eat, Don't forget to share this article with the little robbers, so let's keep their stomachs and health together in a more scientific and healthy way!
