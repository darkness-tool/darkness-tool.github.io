The truth of the potato sprouts: Can you eat it?

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 2 June 2025 Update: 2 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Many people are confused by the fact that the potato sprouts. In our kitchens, potatoes are a common food, but when they begin to germinate, do you hesitate to say, can these budd potatoes eat? In fact, in the bud of potatoes, a toxin known as "garbeth" is released, which requires special attention. Although small amounts of algae do not have a significant impact on the majority of the population, the long-term consumption of sprouts of potatoes may cause poisoning.

Garbage is mainly in the skin and bud of potatoes, especially those that turn green. Therefore, it is important to cut off any budding or greening in the processing of potatoes.

The reason the potato sprouts.

The potato sprouts are usually due to inappropriate saving conditions such as damp or light. When potatoes are affected by these factors, they try to grow, which is their natural reaction. Understanding these reasons will help us better save potatoes and avoid unnecessary waste.

The experts suggest that if the potato sprouts are small and not green around them, they can still be eaten after they have been cut off. However, if the potatoes were green or had a clear gestation, it would be better not to eat.

How can you eat potatoes safely?

To ensure security, the following are suggestions: 2. Periodic inspection of potatoes with a view to removing sprouts or greening. 3. Remove the skin of the potatoes before cooking in order to reduce the alkaline intake. This will effectively reduce health risks.

While it may still be possible to eat, safety is always the most important consideration in the choice. If there is any doubt, it would be better to choose fresh potatoes and avoid ingesting sprouts.

Conclusions
