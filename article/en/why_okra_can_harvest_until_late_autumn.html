Why would akills be collected till late autumn? The miracle of the life of this vegetable and the mystery of growing it.

Author: Handsome. Date of issue: 5 September 2026 Update: 5 September 2026 Reading time: About 9 minutes

Autumn is a wonderful crop that is extremely resilient and has been productive beyond imagination! Many people watch most of the summer vegetables fade during the autumn season, but are surprised to find that the akills in the garden are still green and new flowers and fruits are emerging. What is the reason why Autumn has been so powerfully collected in late autumn?

To understand the super-extension of the autumn, it begins with its natural environment and its natural genes. Autumn plants are produced in the hot zone and are designed to cope with high temperatures and strong sunlight, which makes it extremely rapid in the summer in Taiwan.

Powerful adaptation from the heat belt.

As a result of the warmth and abundant light, the roots of the autumn are deep and strong and are well prepared for drought. And when the heat of summer swept over the earth, many vegetables stopped growing because they could not stand the heat, but the acupuncture was like water, showing amazing growth and vitality.

As the season enters the autumn, the temperature begins to cool, but as long as the sun is full and the average temperature has not fallen, the akills will continue to maintain their basic physical metabolism. This high degree of tolerance for the environment is the first key to its ability to survive beyond the season.

The mystery of continuous harvest.

Autumn itself belongs to the crop of ‘repeated flowers’, in addition to climate adaptation. The hormonal system of plants continues to signal that the mother plant continues to divide the buds, bloom and produce new berries, as long as the ripe nuts are removed.

This "more and more" feature, combined with the proper management of a farmer or gardener's lovers, allows for a significant increase in the original lifetime from summer to late autumn around November.

Farmer and gardener.

In order for the autumn to be well received, it's not enough by its natural skills, as is the cultivation of the day after tomorrow. A number of experienced growers are going to "crystal" before the fall, inhibiting their masters, and encouraging the growth of side branches, so that nutrients can be distributed more effectively to flowers.

In addition, the autumn is a very high-consumable and fertilized crop. After entering the autumn, if phosphates can be filled and leaves cleaned to maintain ventilation in due course, it will be effective in preventing insects and maintaining a strong mother ' s plant so that it can still supply fresh fruits in the autumn wind.

It is because of the heat-resistant genes with both thermal plants, the biological properties of the continuous flowering results, and scientific field management that autumn can break the limits of normal summer vegetables and accompany us through the cold, deep autumn season.

Next time you see an autumn that is still fresh in the middle of the autumn on the market or on your own balcony, you might want to give a little more credit to this lifeless miracle. If you want to experience this high-yielding achievement, write down these little tricks and prepare for the next one!
