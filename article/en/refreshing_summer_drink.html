We must prepare for the summer! Make your own cooler and cooler drinks, so you can cool off at once!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 1 June 2025 Update: 1 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

In the hot summer, how can we not have a cooler drink? Inflammation in the summer, the temperature rises and is often disturbing. At this point, not only is a self-made cooler and cooler drink an effective cure for thirst, but it also brings freshness at once. Today, we will share a simple, easy-to-eat summer recipe that will ease your home-made work and enjoy the cold and delicious.

The main components of this drink are fresh fruit and natural honey, mixed with clean or bubble water, which is healthy and delicious. You can choose different fruits, like lemons, mints, strawberries and so on, so that every mouth is full of surprises.

Material requirements

You need to prepare the following: - Fresh fruit (e.g. lemon, orange, strawberry, etc.) - Natural honey. - Clean or bubble. - Ice (optional)

Make steps

1. Put fresh fruit slices in the cup. 2. Add a suitable amount of honey and adjust the sweetness to the individual population. 3. Plumbing or bubble water. 4. Finally, add ice to make the drink more delicious.

This drink is not only for your own good, but also as a cool drink for your party, so that friends can share this summer. I'd like to invite my friends to a DIY drink party after hot lunch and add to the fun!

Change multi-end formulation

In addition to the basic formulations, you can try to add other components, such as mint leaves, pretzels or coconuts, to make the taste of drinks more diverse. Every production, as season changes, adjusts according to the season's fruit, and every cup is a fresh surprise!

Health tip

This drink, which is not only delicious, but also rich in vitamins and antioxidants, can help you refill water and increase your immunity. In summer, especially, good water intake is essential for health.

Make this cool and cool drink, so that you can enjoy nature's freshness in the heat of the summer at all times.

Conclusion

Let's try this one of your own. Whether it's for yourself or for your friends, it's a new taste for your summer. Keep cool and enjoy every summer after noon!
