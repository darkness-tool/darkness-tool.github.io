According to biologists, bees are uniquely designed. When a worker bee feels threatened, it instinctively attacks with abdomen. On the contrary, when the bees try to fly, the needles are stuck. Then, along with the poison bag and part of the body, it tore off the bee. The process resulted in severe internal damage, and bees usually die of blood loss and organ failure within minutes. This "suicide attack" is a unique feature of bees, and other needles, such as bees, can be used again. Scientists think this is the result of millions of years of evolution, ensuring the safety of the bees.

The lethal design of a bee needle.

Let's consider the structure of the bee needle in more detail. The needles are made up of three parts: needles, poison bags and hooks. The venom is wrapped in needles that cause pain and venom during injection. The capsules continue to pump the toxins, even if the bee needles fall and they run for minutes. Biologists have pointed out that this system has greatly increased the efficiency of the bee's defences. Despite the huge loss of one body, it is worth the investment for the whole hive. Imagine how strong a little bee's life is for the safety of thousands of people. This explains why the herd is always indestructible in the face of predators.
