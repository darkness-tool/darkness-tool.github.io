The creepy truth! Why does a dog always have to eat shit? What about the Elvis?

Author: Arrested Date of issue: 26 September 2026 Update: 26 September 2026 Reading time: About 7 minutes

The moment of the collapse of every lord, no more than when the beloved dog "finished" and turned his back and swallowed his own shit! The scene is often a sign of a stomach flip and a suspicion of life. People often laugh that "dogs can't eat shit," but behind this sludicrous phrase is a deep biological evolution and physical code. And even more interesting is that, being one of humanity's favorite furry children, a cold, elegant cat simply ignores such behaviour. What is the hidden secret in this?

In veterinary science, dog-feeding has a famous term called "poaching." Many people think that dogs are sick or that they lack important nutrition. In fact, the reason for this hair-skinned behavior is far more complicated than expected, covering the physical, mental and ancient instincts left behind.

Reason one: Alive instincts and maternity responsibilities inherited from the ancestors

To understand why dogs like to eat shit, we have to go back to ancient times. Their ancestors lived in the wild of the crisis before they were fertilized by humans. When a bitch gives birth to a baby dog, in order to prevent the smell of defecation from attracting enemies and predators, the dog eats all of its excreta, thereby keeping the nest clean and safe. Some of the genes are still deeply imprinted in the DNA of modern-day domestic dogs.

In addition to the cleaning instincts of the dogs, the pups are in the prime of months to explore the world. They like to taste their mouths and touch everything new around them. If, during the pups' time, the owner is surprised and rushing to find out about the shit, some smart doggies assume that it's a game with him, and even eat it so they can "exhaust evidence."

Reason two: Insufficiency and greed in nutrition

Another common reason is the problem of nutrition and digestion. If a dog eats bad fermentation, or if the stomach digests fermentate, which results in the protein and fat in the food not being fully absorbed by the body, then for a dog with a good sense of smell, the shit actually smells less like the fresh food. Since they smell like food, they will naturally eat as a snack.

In addition, some of the dog species are born super stomach kings with a strong desire for food. As long as they have access, they won't let go. It also increases their chances of trying to eat shit.

Reason three: Stress, anxiety and boredom

In addition to physical and instinctal factors, mental factors are also essential. When dogs are kept in small spaces for a long time, lack enough exercise, or are seriously anxious and bored because their owners are away from home for a long time, they may develop unusual coercion to divert attention, and eating shit is often one of the ways to relieve stress.

Why don't cats eat shit?

After seeing the dog, you must be wondering why the noble cats and cats are always so good at hygiene and cleaning, don't say they eat shit, and they're always sore when they see the excrement not buried. This is mainly because cats' ancestors are sole predators from the desert. In order not to expose themselves in a volatile environment, cats are born with strong feces instincts.

More importantly, cats are strict predators with a high demand for protein, and the evolution of their digestive systems has led them to the highest standards of their taste. Cats are not only extremely sensitive to the smell of their own excrement, they are simply not able to endure an unclean environment. In their world, much of the waste that must be completely isolated and buried is completely off-limits to "food".

In conclusion, while the dog eats shit that makes humans feel weird and disgusting, it is mostly a call for help from instinct, nutrition or psychological needs. As long as the master adjusts the diet, fills the fermentation, increases the time of company and takes the right behavior training, it will help the child slowly change this awkward little habit!
