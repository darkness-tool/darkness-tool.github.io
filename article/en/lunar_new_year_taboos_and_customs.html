Do you dare to commit the taboos of New Year's? I've been poor all my life!

Author: Keung Date of issue: 24 January 2026 Update: 24 January 2026 Reading time: About 9 minutes

The New Year's ban on agriculture has had a devastating effect on the situation! Every time the new year of the agricultural calendar comes, the families of their families will be happy to welcome the new year with lights, springs and firecrackers. However, behind this joy lies the taboo that has been passed down by countless ancestors, who, if accidentally touched, are said to have caused year-round loss of luck, loss of wealth and even family discord! These taboos. New Year's Eve continued until the 15th of the month, covering food, food, cleaning, words, behaviour, etc., which has led many modern young people to speak out. Today, we're going to go into detail about these traditional taboos and make you happy and happy!

First, from New Year's Eve. New Year's Eve was the most important round-the-clock of the year, and the whole family sat around for an annual dinner, which could never be left alone. There's gotta be fish on the table. It's gonna be more than a year, but don't eat any fish! It must be left behind. It means there's plenty of luck and money. If the fish are not carefully eaten, it is said that the next year will be short of money to spend, so poor that it will cry. Many elders will see people eat all of the fish, and they will stop right away, afraid of bad luck for the whole year.

The worst nine taboos of the beginning of the year.

At the beginning of the year, the first day of the new year, taboos were the most severe. First of all, it is not possible to clean the land, because it is the cleaning of the land that is the cleaning of the family's wealth and good luck, and the taking out of the waste is the dumping of the blessing. Many families have finished cleaning the brooms before New Year's Eve and never move in the first to the fifth. If you really have to do it, you have to do it from outside the door.

Secondly, first-year baths, hair washing and laundry are not allowed. Because it's Water God's birthday, water washes away wealth. There are places where hair can't be washed even for the first two years. The elders have often said that one day before the year, they must wash their bodies, wash away the bad air of a year, and that after the year, they will no longer be able to use the `wash'.

Again, nothing can be broken, especially the cups. Breaking things means being broke and broken, and if you do not break them, you will immediately say "Flowing, Glory and Glory" or "Peace and security of life". Moreover, shears and needles cannot be moved because the sharp objects are controversial and talkative, and must be avoided in the course of the year so as not to continue fighting throughout the year.

In terms of words, be careful not to say unlucky words, such as "death," "disease", "poor", "break" and "lost." Even the words "end" and "light" sound neutral. The elders taught me good luck for the first year and bad luck for the first time. They can't even scold the children, because the first time they get scolded, they get scolded all year round.

You can't ignore the special taboos of other days.

In the second year, it is usually the day the daughter is married and returned to his mother's house, commonly known as the son-in-law's day. The day could not be a fight and could not lend money to anyone else because it represented the loan of money. The third year is a "Bloody Day" and it's called a "Blod Dog Day" and it's easy to speak, so it's a tradition not to go out to worship so that it doesn't piss off.

The first five is the "Fifty Five" and many taboos come to this day to be truly lifted. The day can begin to empty the garbage, clean the land, and in some places firecrackers will be used against the poor God. Businesses have often chosen to open their first five markets, marking a new year of booming business.

In addition, there are widespread taboos: not to take a nap because it is lazy for a year; not to take medication (unless it is urgent) because it is sick; not to wear black and white, because black and white are the colours of mourning and can be bad luck. Even some places can't sleep too early for the first time, fearing that the year will pass.

These taboos, though they may sound exaggerated, reflect the good will of the ancients to pray for peace, to be rich and to avoid evil. Moderns may not be in full compliance, but knowledge of these traditions can make the New Year more cultural and avoid narration.

The New Year of Agriculture is one of the most important festivals of Chinese culture, and these taboos have been passed on from generation to generation to remind us of the need to be cautious in our new year, to cherish our families and to focus on good fortune. Let's hope you remember these traditions and let the new year really flourish and make money! If you've accidentally broken your taboos, don't worry. Just change your heart. Happy New Year.
