It's amazing to know that the legend of the beasts was a modern creation a hundred years ago? New Year's Old Testament has caused a lot of trouble!

Author: Da Ming Date of issue: 24 January 2026 Update: 24 January 2026 Reading time: About 8 minutes

This is the truth that will completely reverse what you know about the Nen beast! Every spring festival, the story of the beast is told countless times: a long time ago, the legend said, there was a strange beast called the Year, who came out every year on New Year's Eve to do evil, but it was afraid of loud, red and fire. As a result, every year at the end of the year, people at home are cleaning up, putting Red Unions, firecrackers and red lights in order to scare the "age beasts" away. However, this widespread legend is not an ancient myth, but a modern invention of less than a century of history.

The true nature of the Year: nothing to do with monsters.

The first thing to learn about the truth is to go back to the nature of the word "year". The word "year" appeared in Oracle, written as an upper-earth man, meaning that the grain was ripe. “The year is about the maturity of all grains throughout the year. In other words, the original meaning of the Year has nothing to do with monsters, but is a word of acceptance and praise. "I don't see myself for three years. The term "years" at this time has led to the concept of the span of the years. Three Years of Warfare, "One year, all the grains are ripe for another year." As the grain matures, it takes a week, the "year" evolves into a time unit. "The Year of the Nat, the Year of the Sun, the Year of the Choon, the Year of Tang's Day." It is clear from the ancients that the "years" have been written, but they have nothing to do with the beast. In the time of the South Koreans, The Year of the Year of the Jubilee, "The house of the late, the house of the house of the house of the late, the house of the house of the late, the place of the year of the new year, and the gathering of the people to drink." And the food of rest till the twelveth of the New Year, and the street is set aside, and he thinks he will come to Xinjiang." As can be seen, in ancient literature, the meaning of "years" has never been recorded in any way related to "years" since the beginning of time, the harvest, the sacrifice.

The old beast's legend was born in modern times.

So, where did the old beast legend come from? The first reference to the "Yen Beast" was made in a national newspaper in the early 1930s. On January 17, 1933, Shanghai writer Zhengyu Mei's newspaper, The Diamonds, recorded an article written by Sun Yu's voice, " The memory of old times and old times " . It is written: "A man with a mausoleum of purple microstellars, each of which is painted by a stone, and which is locked like a dog or a beast, or a cloud in the name of an animal, or a cloud in the name of an animal, which is often a desire to eat human beings, and which is locked up in such a way as not to cause evil to the lower world, so that it is no more desirable than a year. And when it is gone, there is no knowledge of its provenance, and it is not worthy of witness. This is the first known text about the animals. It was not every other year that the "year-old beast" came out to eat, but only to mention that it wanted to eat, so he was guarded by a purple microstar. The name "year" of the animal is just a relatively small, unknown view, the dominant view being that the painter actually paints a purple microstar and a star.

On 1 February 1935, another piece of Sun Yu ' s Spoken, published in the newspaper " The Diamonds of the King " , added: "Remember the old words of the father, and the young beasts, like lions and not lions, like dogs and not dogs, eaters of the night, painters paint purple microstars, the beasts locked in the pillars, are the same thing. That is also the saying of those who are superstitious, who do not stand in the midst of a new age, and think that they are obstinate. Even though they have not been told, have not been tested, or have spoken in the name of the Zidong wildlings, they are the beasts of the year, and they are similar to the social outlook of the sun. There is a constant danger of life in the eyes of the poor, fearing more than the beast before it, and hardly anointing its blood kisses. Here, Sun Yu’s statement clearly states that the legend of the animals may come from the metaphor of the “year-old” pressure, such as the collection of debts at the end of the year, becoming a monster. On December 31, 1939, the newspaper published an article under the name "Sen" "Years of the Year's Legend" which for the first time described in detail the end of the year and the second night of the year when the animal would eat, and linked it directly to the concept of "years of the year."

The changing and popular image of animals.

The image of the animals is becoming rich and diverse in the afterlife. The Iron One Veterinary has a large, first-born animal. In the Grand Public Journal, the Year of the Year of the Year Examination, it is known that the animals have white hair and that there are pink and white footprints. In the New People's Daily News, the story of the year is that the animals grow with two heads, four ears and eight legs. People's Daily New Year's Adventists and Courages refer to the bloodbath of the animals. It was not until 16 February 1980, in the Daily People ' s Journal, that the article " The legends and customs of the New Year " , published by Zhao Zhou Zhou Zhou Zhongxing, the People ' s Daily, systematically linked the distinctive points of the fear of the red animals, the fear of light, the fear of the sound, and the practice of pasting the Red Union, the firing of firecrackers and the lighting of light, became a popular version of the legends of the beasts that have existed so far. This article has an unforgettable impact on the strength of the story of the pro-age beast.

The popularity of the old animal legend has largely benefited from the dissemination of modern media and the codification of folklore. In the spring of 1981, Guangzhou City's popular art gallery published a book entitled The book of Spring Festivals, which talks about the origins of Spring Festival and legends, has further amplified its impact by presenting the "years" as a collection of legends based on historical data such as ancients and antiquities. After that, the story of the animals of the year has been fashioned into children's books, cartoons, such as the 1981 fairytale "The New Year's Eve" story, the 1984 paper clipping "The New Year's Eve story" and so on, making the image of the animals an integral part of the Spring Festival culture.

The cultural meaning of the old animal legend.

Although the old beast's legends are not ancient, they concoct in a series of anti-terror customs (e.g. firecrackers, Red Unions, red lights, age watch, etc.) to a complete and telling interpretation system that gives them a more dynamic cultural content. It transforms abstract pressure and fear of the unknown into a monster that can be understood, challenged and even expelled, reflecting the Chinese desire for a peaceful and happy life. It is a "new tradition" that is deeply embedded in the memory of China's Spring Festival. It does not diminish our New Year's fun, but rather allows us to understand more deeply the rich cultural and historical dimensions of spring practice.
