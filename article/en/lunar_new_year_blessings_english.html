Every new year of the agricultural calendar, the Chinese world is filled with joy and joy, and people worship each other, say good words, and pray for peace and prosperity in the coming year. The traditional hymns, such as "Congratulations on the Rich", "All things will be well" and "all the years will be long" are all classics of good will. This article will provide you with a detailed translation and usage of English, which is often used to see the New Year of Agriculture, so that you can give you a full blessing in the face of foreign friends or the international arena.

The New Year of Agriculture, also known as Spring Festival or Chinese New Year/Lunar New Year, is the Chinese's most important traditional holiday. The festival is not just a tradition of round-ups, firecrackers, and spring union, but even less a blessing to congratulate each other. These words often have good connotations, such as wealth, health, peace, success, etc. Translating them into English not only makes non-Chinese people aware of our traditional culture, but also adds to the joy of cross-cultural communication.

The best agricultural year of the year.

First, "Happy New Year" is the most popular blessing. In English, it is possible to say "Happy Lunar New Year!" or "Happy Chinese New Year!" which is more neutral and suitable for all Asian countries using their agricultural calendars, and then more politicizing human culture. Another very popular expression is "Gong Xi Fa Cai!", which is a translation of "Congratulations and Wealth!" or "Congratulations and be prosperous!"

In addition to the basic questions, many traditional good words are echoed in English. May there be a surplus every year! or "Wishing you throughout every year."

May wealth come broadly to you!, or May money and resources be perfect!, and "May wealth come to you!" or "Wishing you economy and treasures." The other sentence of Superman's "Fifty at the Gate" means that the five blessings of longevity, wealth, health, virtue and good will come to you!

Good health and safety and success.

"May you ride on the credit of success!" or "Wishing you great goals."

I wish you good luck in the year ahead. These blessing words not only add to the joy, but also give recipients a full sense of positive energy.
