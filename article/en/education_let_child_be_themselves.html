The child is not a replica of you!

I'm sorry. Date of issue: 23 November 2025 Update: 23 November 2025 Reading time: About 4 minutes

If a child lives his whole life in the framework of who you should be, he will never know who he really is! In today's society, parents expect more and more of their children from birth than powdered milk, bean-in-law, talent and famous schools. However, a growing number of psychologists and education specialists find that children who have lost their smile at the top of their grades are often forced to take a “not their own path” when they are young. Dr. Silvana Montenairo, President of the International Association of Montessoli, who was nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize, said: "The first priority in education is not to teach children, but to help them find out who they are. "The real education is not to shape the child into what the parents think, but to observe, like gardeners, the characteristics of each plant, giving it the most suitable light, water and soil. When children can really be themselves, they will not only be happy, but also show amazing potential and creativity. This paper will show you that the "self-education" at the forefront of the world's education will not be lost, but will release the most unique self. Many families have proven that when parents let go, the children go further and more powerful. It's not just forgiving, it's for science and love, for the children to find the starway that belongs to him. Ready? Let's break the shackles of traditional education together!

Wrong education: turning a child into the shadow of a parent

And many Asian parents used to say, “I eat more salt than you eat, and I walk more bridges than you, and I am good for you.” The doctor ' s parents therefore wanted the child to study medicine, the engineer ' s parents wanted the child to study science and the business parents wanted the child to succeed. Children have been put into remedial classes, talent classes since they were young and have been scheduled on holidays without even having time to go to bed. On the face of it, children look good, but their heart is full of anxiety, stress and even depression and self-defeating. Taiwan Child and Adolescent Mental Health Institute estimates that the proportion of high school students seeking psychological support for their advancement to higher education has grown over 300 per cent in the last decade. The 80-year-old Harvard School of Happiness Tracking found that the most important factor affecting human well-being in life is not wealth, fame, but "can you be yourself." When children are forced to live in the expectations of others, they gradually lose their self-identification, and when they grow up, they often feel empty. Many senior executives suddenly collapsed when they were 40, often because "I never knew what I really wanted." This is the "shadow syndrome" — a dream that children have been chasing their parents all their lives, but never their own. To break this vicious cycle, parents have to change their mind: a child is not your thing, he is an independent soul.

What is the education of "make your child yourself"?

In short, it's to give "choice" back to the children, so they can explore, try, find interest and talent. The first secret of Finnish education worldwide is to start formal schooling at the age of seven, before it was full of games and nature. The WWSF education emphasizes: protection of senses at ages 0-7, nurturing emotions at ages 7-14, and rational development after age 14, so that children grow up by their natural rhythm. Montesoli, on the other hand, says, "Follow the child," and the teacher, like an observer, prepares the environment for the child to choose what he wants to do. The top private school in the U.S., "Acton Academy", has no books, no exams, and the children run their own companies, do their own projects, but they get into Stanford, Harvard. These leading educational systems have one thing in common: they believe that every child is born with a unique gift, and that education is "discovering" rather than "filling." When children do what they like and are good at every day, they enter what the psychologist Cheksen Mihari calls "heart flow" and learns 5-10 times as efficiently as traditional education. More importantly, they will create a strong sense of self-effectiveness and know that "I am capable of taking charge of my life." This confidence, more than any famous school halo, will make children more stable in the future. Because in the AI era, the ability to recite and test is no longer valuable, and what is really scarce is creativity, self-mobilization and uniqueness. And these powers can only be awakened in the process of becoming themselves.

What can parents do? Eight practical ways.

1. Leave the child with at least one hour of "dry time" per day to find his own work, which is the cradle of creativity. To reduce the number of talent classes so that the child chooses one or two items that he really likes and allows him to give up. When the child says, "I don't want to read a book", he asks, "What do you want?" instead of immediately blaming. 4. Establishment of a family council system to involve children from an early age in family decision-making and practice expression and responsibility. 5. To encourage the child to write "Happy Diaries": to write about the happiest thing today on a daily basis and help him to see the true pleasures. 6. Bringing the child into a diverse domain: museums, farms, theatres, street performances, so that he can naturally discover interest. 7. Allowing the child to fail, even encouraging him to deliberately fail once, telling him that “failure is the quickest way to find the right path”. 8. Parents have taken the lead and bravely pursued their interests so that their children can see what it is like to live. A mother who did these things shared the idea that his son had become a home-based electric campaign anchor from the junior master of the remedial class, and that his 19-year-old income was already millions. She said, “I gave him the right to choose, but he went further than I had planned.” I'm sorry. When parents want to let go, children tend to surprise you by proving that he was born a unique legend. True love, not control, but full children become himself.

Conclusion: May every child become its hero.

The future world does not need more copies, but more original souls. When we're willing to let go of "I'm good for you" control, we really give our children the greatest gift -- to be free. Perhaps the child will not be a doctor, a lawyer, an engineer, but it doesn’t matter, he may be the next YouTube, game designer, a permanent farmer, or whatever he loves. And that man who loves to work, who wakes up with power every day, is the greatest success. Remember, "You are raising not a "beautiful child" but an independent body that will change the world." May you let go and let the child fly to his sky. For when he was himself, he turned to you and said, "Thank you for not turning me into another you, but for making me who I am now." This will be the most exciting thanks you have ever heard in your life. From now on, let's practice together: replace "You should" with "You want to?" – this will change a child's life.
