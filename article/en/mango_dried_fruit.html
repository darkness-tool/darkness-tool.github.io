Fancy buds! Teach you how to make delicious mango dry!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 3 June 2025 Update: 3 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

The process of making mango dry is simple but fun! Mango is one of the most popular fruits in the hot summer. However, making mango is a dry fruit that can last longer and is unique in taste and taste. Today, we will teach you how to make delicious mango drys at home. You can enjoy this healthy snack with a few simple steps. Whether it's a snack or breakfast, mango dry is the perfect choice. Now, let's see how it's done! Prepare your food and tools, let's do it! The recipe is very suitable for family production and sharing with relatives.

First, we need to choose mature mangoes, so we can guarantee a sweet taste. Skin the mangoes, then cut them into a smoulder, about 0.5 centimetres thick. This thickness ensures that mangoes are hot in the dry process. Next, the choice is to immerse mangoes in lemonade, which not only prevents oxidation but also adds to the flavor. After about 10 minutes of immersion, the mangos are dried and ready for drying. If you like, you can add a little sugar to make mango dry sweeter. This step can be adjusted according to the population. It's worth mentioning that using fresh food makes the finished food more delicious!

Dry mango steps.

Put ready mangos on the grill and make sure there's enough space between them. Next, the oven is preheated to 60°C (140°F), the best temperature for dry mangoes. Put the oven in the oven and bake for about six to eight hours. During the baking process, mangoes can be checked every other time to ensure that they are not too dry. When mangoes become soft and no longer damp, they are made to cool. After cooling, the mango drys are kept in a sealed bag, which can extend its shelf life. Patience is the key to the process of making mango drys, so let's wait for the moment when the fragrance blows. At this point, you'll find the mangos that you've made are sweet and unresistible!

In the case of home-made mango drying, all kinds of drinks can be combined and tasted. Tea, juice or milk are the perfect mix. In addition, mango drys can be used for dessert, like mango dry pudding or cake, which is unique. Such homemade snacks, not only healthy, but also delicious at home. In addition, the production process is full of fun, sharing joy with family. Remember to film the production and share your achievements with your friends! Small skills and hearts

In the process of producing mango dry, it is important to choose fresh materials, which directly affects the final taste. If you want to add flavor, you can try to add other spices, such as cinnamon or vanilla. It'll make your mango dry even more impressive. In addition, cooled mango drys can be stored in a cool and dry place, with a continuous taste. If you're worried about the high distribution of sugar, you can adjust it to the individual population, and you can choose the way it is made without sugar.
