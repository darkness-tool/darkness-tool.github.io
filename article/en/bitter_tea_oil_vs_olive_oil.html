It's amazing to see that the health of bitter tea oil is better than olive oil!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 1 June 2025 Update: 1 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

In the tide of healthy food, bitter tea oil is becoming a new star! This oil from the tea tree is not only unique, but also rich in a variety of body-friendly ingredients. With more and more studies proving the health benefits of bitter tea oil, many are beginning to wonder whether it should be considered an alternative to olive oil.

The unique taste of bitter tea oil makes it an excellent choice for cooking. Whether it's cold or hot, bitter tea oil can add a special fragrance to cooking. Many cooks begin to include it in their daily cooking, creating amazing and delicious food.

The nutrients of bitter tea oil.

Suffering tea oil is rich in a variety of fatty acids, especially unsaturated units and fatty acids, which help improve cardiovascular health. In addition, bitter tea oil contains a wealth of antioxidants, such as tea polyphenol, that can effectively combat free radicals and reduce ageing.

Compared to olive oil, the smoke spot of bitter tea oil is higher, which means that it is not easy to produce harmful substances in high-temperature cooking, suitable for all types of cooking, from fried to baked. This makes bitter tea oil more dynamic in the kitchen.

Health benefits

Studies show that regular consumption of bitter tea oil can help reduce cholesterol levels, promote blood circulation and reduce the risk of heart disease. A number of experts suggested that bitter tea oil should be added to the diet to improve overall health.

In addition, bitter tea oil has good care for skin. It's deep-seated, dry and rough, and can even be used as a natural skin. These advantages make bitter tea oil not just for food, but also for beauty care.

How to select and save

When choosing bitter tea oil, you should choose the products that are produced by cold pressure, which keeps more nutrients. When saved, it should be placed in a cool and dry place to avoid direct sunlight to extend its shelf life.

In your daily diet, try to replace the tea with olive oil and add new flavors and health benefits to your food. The bitter tea oil can bring you unexpectedly good food, whether it be salad, fried or baked!

Conclusions

The bitter tea oil, with its unique taste and health benefits, is increasingly becoming an important option for people to eat. Whether in terms of nutrients or culinary activity, bitter tea oil is not less than olive oil, and it is worth every housewife and culinary lover.
