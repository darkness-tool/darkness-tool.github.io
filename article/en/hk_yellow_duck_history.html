The twilight of Hong Kong's yellow ducklings

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 3 June 2025 Update: 3 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Let's look back at this big, childish duck! Hong Kong ' s yellow duck, which was first seen in 2013, quickly became a symbol of the city. The rubber duck, designed by Dutch artist François Ahen, attracted thousands of tourists and citizens to take photographs. It's not just an art, it's a memory of childhood. With the arrival of the ducks, Hong Kong's seaport has become more dynamic, adding a lot of color to the city. The size of the duck is huge, 16.5 metres high, which is amazing. Every time it shakes on the water, it makes the passers-by smiles and cries. The presence of this duck has encouraged people to discuss and think about art. It also allows many families to take their children to the sea and enjoy their rare years of parenthood. Photos of ducks in social media have become a hot topic. We're all alone with this giant duck.

The first show by ducks attracted over 1 million viewers and became one of the most popular artistic devices in Hong Kong in recent years. Photos were taken by ducks and social media were shared, making ducks quickly becoming cyber-red. A number of businesses have also taken the opportunity to launch the goods associated with ducks, creating a little bit of duck. With the success of the ducks, the arts community in Hong Kong began to focus more on the development of public art. It is not just an exhibition of art, but a symbol of community cohesion. Many citizens have started to focus on the meaning of public art and are actively involved in related activities. The idea of a duck's design has also aroused many voices, beginning to think about how to make the city more friendly. During the presence of ducks, many volunteers were also involved in cleaning the seaports and demonstrating a sense of social responsibility. The story of ducky tells us that art is not only in the gallery, but also in our lives.

Duck's influence.

The success of the duck is not limited to Hong Kong, but is also displayed in many cities around the world as a bridge between international cultural exchanges. In every new city, ducks cause a strong reaction from the local population. At each of the exhibition sites, ducks bring new cultural views to the area. Many people have become interested in public art because of ducks and are participating in various artistic activities. The story of ducks has also generated numerous innovations on the Internet, and many designers have been inspired by it. It has become an important element in the promotion of art education, and many schools have started holding creative competitions related to ducks. The demonstration of ducks is not only an exhibition of art, but also an economic development that attracts a large number of tourists. Many restaurants have launched duck-themed menus that allow tourists to enjoy their food while also experiencing art. The impact of ducklings is everywhere, allowing people to re-examine the good things in life. It tells us that art can deepen human connections.

In addition to the impact of art, ducks also bring the message of environmental protection to the attention of the ocean. Many environmental groups use the visibility of ducks to promote ocean protection. The presence of ducks reminds us that it is everyone's responsibility to protect the environment. On the day of the duck show, many citizens volunteered to participate in the seaside clean-up, making the environment more beautiful. The ducks became the perfect link between the city environment and public art, and people saw the power of art. It reminds everyone that art can bring positive change to society. Every show of this duck is a rethinking of life and the environment. As the story of the ducks spread, more and more people became involved in environmental action. It's not just a symbol of art, it's a desire for beauty in everyone's heart.

Future prospects

In the future, ducks will continue their journey, show them in more cities and bring joy and art to more people. Each show is a new story and looks to ducks in different cultures. We look forward to seeing ducks in the cities of the future, bringing more surprises to people. The duck has become a common theme of global art lovers, and the future will be even more difficult. The story of ducks reminds us that, wherever we are, art and life are connected. It makes us understand that simple things can also bring endless happiness. Duck's journey continues and looks forward to its every appearance. I hope everyone who sees ducks will find their own share of the fun.
