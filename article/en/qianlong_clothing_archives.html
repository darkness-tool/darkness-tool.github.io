Discovering the Emperor's closet!

Author: Da Ming Date of issue: 8 September 2026 Update: 8 September 2026 Reading time: About 7 minutes

What does the emperor wear every day? This secret file tells you the answer! In the palaces of the Forbidden City, the Emperor's clothing and food journey has always been top secret. However, through the court files retained by Qing Dynasty, the latter were able to see clearly the details of the emperor ' s life. Among them, it is a priceless historical treasure that records the Emperor's dress in the Clothes.

The so-called Clothes File is an original file of the Emperor's daily dress, dress change, clothing reward and festivities, which is used by the Qing Dynasty Department Office and Divisions. These documents contain not only the quality, colour and quantity of the clothing, but also the extremely strict ritual system of the court.

The Dry Fashion in Clothes: A Tight Clothes System

As the monarch of the Qing Dynasty, the Emperor's garment represented the highest aesthetic and power symbol of the Empire. As can be seen from the description of The Clothes, each of the Emperor's clothes is not on a casual basis, but must be changed in strict compliance with the accelerator and the field. For example, when to wear a sweater and when to change a cotton dress, there are clear time nodes and regulations in the file.

Qing Dynasty's dress has integrated the cultural identity of the Han. On the one hand, the costumes retain horse-and-shoe and arrow-sleeved sleeves, reminding the Manchurian nobles of the tradition of "right away"; on the other hand, they are integrated into the traditional Chinese culture of Chinese Chinese garnets and show a high and normal status.

The extreme display of luxury.

The most amazing is the number of fabrics and the work that can be done annually in Gangnam's trifling (Kang Ning, Suzhou, Hangzhou). The dragon robes, coats, crowns and ornaments contained in the file were used for the top-of-the-art work of silk, weavers and embroidery, using the hearts and minds of many of the top craftsmen.

For example, the most representative of dragon robes, which usually have nine five-capture gold dragons, are accompanied by five-coloured clouds and twelve tampons of all things in the universe. These complex designs are not only a test of the skills of the embroidery master, but also of the gold wires and the natural dyes used by them, as well as of the royal luxuries.

From the file, the emperor's daily life and reward.

In addition to documenting the Emperor's own clothes, the Clothes also contains details of the Emperor's reward for the clothes of his wife, son and servant. Every time a major celebration or post-celebrity remains, the file contains a precise list. These records reflect not only the hierarchies of the harem, but also the cleavages of the emperor under the weight of grace.

These seemingly plethora of day-to-day water bills are in fact important building blocks of research into the Qing Dynasty Economics, milling, art history, and by-laws. Every one of these is a concrete microcosm of the strong and highly centralized rule of the Great Qing Empire.

Through the window of history, Clothes, we have been able to travel hundreds of years to see the court life of the Emperor. These precious contributions not only preserve the most valuable historical truth for future generations, but also keep the radiance of the charisma of the Qing Dynasty Court in modern times.
