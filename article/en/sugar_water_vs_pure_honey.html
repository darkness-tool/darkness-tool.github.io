What's the difference between sugar and regular honey? Don't waste any more of your money on sugar!

Author: Fan Date issued: 10 September 2026 Update: 10 September 2026 Reading time: About 4 minutes

Many people think that natural honey is just "higher sugar" and that's a huge misunderstanding! In our daily lives, we often use honey bubble water as a food drink or add a taste to dessert. However, the market is full of cheap prices and honey or poor-quality syrup, and some even replace it with white sugar. In fact, white sugar water differs from regular natural honey in composition, structure and value for human health.

Chemically, the main component of sugar is sugar cane, a double sugar, which must be broken into single sugar (glucose and fruit sugar) by the transformation enzymes in the human digestive system before it can be absorbed. If the human metabolic system is weak, or if there is too much sugar, it can easily cause a physical burden. In contrast, the normal natural honey is a vegetative honey taken by bees, made of fully fabricated complications, whose main components are the single sugars of glucose and fruit sugar, which can be absorbed directly by humans without a complex process of digestion.

Essential differences in nutrient values

Sugar water, apart from providing short-term heat, contains almost no nutrients, often referred to in school as "air heat". The proper honey is the essence of nature and contains, in addition to the abundance of glucose and fruit sugar, a variety of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, active enzymes and antioxidants. These natural active ingredients can help to adjust stomach function, promote metabolism, and even have natural antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties, which are too simple to see.

Because of their completely different composition, their metabolic pathways to humans are very different. White sugar is used to cause a rapid rise in blood sugar, with greater pressure on blood sugar control; and high-quality natural honey, with higher sugar, has a more moderate effect on blood sugar. In addition, the unique aromatics and organic acids in honey give a rich sense of taste and It's a natural fragrance that can never be imitated by artificial fragrance.

How can you see the white sugar and the fake honey?

As a result of the higher price of natural honey, many non-squares use sugar to make syrup to impersonate honey. There are a few simple ways to distinguish between real and fake: The first is to observe the appearance and the quality of the honey, which appears to be thicker and more luminous and less luminous, because of the presence of trace ingredients such as pollen, and which is usually clear and transparent. The second is smelling, with natural fragrances of plants in the real honey belts, and fake honeys, often with a single snout of sweets or sugar odour.

We can do more with water mixing. When honey is mixed in cold water, real honey melts in water with a small and lasting bubble, and the water is slightly mixed, while fake honey made of white sugar or molasses creates a large and easily disappearing foam, which dissolves very quickly. In addition, when they provoke real honey with chopsticks, they pull out long, bulletproof silks, which automatically condensate into a sphere when the flow breaks, all of which are physical characteristics that white sugar cannot do.

In conclusion, sugar water is merely a synthetic sweetener, while regular natural honey is a natural nutrient that collects and treats the whole body. In the next selection of bee products, be careful not to try to buy low-quality sugar with an unknown composition at a cheap price. It's a sign of faith that you can really eat with peace and health and that every drop of good honey will bring you real nourishment.
