Taco-cooked recipes! Make delicious snacks at home!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 2 June 2025 Update: 2 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Tacocake is a traditional Chinese snack! The dish is well known for its soft taste and unique scent, often in teahouses and family gatherings. Today we will share with you a simple, easy-to-eat taco-cake recipe that you can enjoy at home!

Material requirements

To make taco cake, you need the following materials: - 300 grams of taro. - 200 grams of sticky rice powder. - 100 grams of sugar. - 500 ml of water. - 1/2 tea spoons. - A tea spoon of oil.

Make steps

1. First, take the taro out of the skin and cut it into small pieces, then evaporate for about 20 minutes until it becomes soft. 2. Put the steamed taro in the bowl, with sugar and salt, and crush it into mud. 3. In the other bowl, the sticky rice powder and water are mixed, evenly mixed and then mixed with taro mud.

4. Then, the mixed paste was poured into the oily voltage, and it was gently flatted. 5. Put the steamer into the steam pan and evaporate it with a medium fire for about 30 minutes until the surface becomes smooth.

6. When it is evaporated, let it cool and then cut into small pieces. 7. The choice is to fry the cut potato cake to the surface of gold, which will be even more delicious!

Access

Tacocake can be hot or cold, with some soy sauce or chili sauce. You can use it as a snack for breakfast or tea and as a guide!

Little Chest.

1. Other ingredients, such as mushrooms or shrimp, may be added according to personal preferences. If you like sweeter, you can increase the use of sugar.

Conclusion

Here's our taco-cake recipe. I hope you'll make delicious taco-cakes at home to surprise families and friends. Come and try!
