Discover the secret inside of the bee! Why can't sugar-fed honey be sold as pure honey?

Author: Fan Date issued: 10 September 2026 Update: 10 September 2026 Reading time: about 5 minutes

This is the truth of a bee-keeping world that has puzzled and shocked many consumers. When you buy honey on the market, you want to buy 100% of your natural honey. However, the fact that many people hear that beehouses feed bees with sugar is questionable: whether honey that feed is pure or not? Why can't this kind of honey sell on the market?

First, we have to understand why beekeepers feed bee sugar. In the face of severe winter or an empty window in the wild with no honey at all, if there is no artificial resupply at all, the hives are likely to die from hunger. Thus, the main purpose of feeding sugar is to sustain the lives and basic survival of the bees in a bad environment, which is a necessary means of bee-rearing.

Where is the conflict between sugar and honey?

The question is about time and time. When spring comes and the season of nature begins, professional beekeepers will make a very crucial move to stop feeding immediately. Sugar, and a way to empty the spleen of sugar, to make sure the bees collect pure honey.

However, if some operators do not actually clean up, or if, in order to increase the production of honey, they still feed white sugar during the honey season or overtime, bees move the sugar water and store it in their nests, and the product material is "black sugar thaw honey" rather than real natural nectar.

Why can't this honey sell as pure honey?

In terms of components, the main components of natural honey are single sugar, which contains glucose and fruit sugar, and is rich in a wide range of active substances such as vitamins, minerals, methamic acid and yeast. On the contrary, sugar, which is based on white sugar, contains a large amount of sugar and cane, with a significantly reduced nutrient value, and does not provide the health benefits that natural honey deserves.

More importantly, in the food laws and national standards, honey is strictly defined as a bee that collects the sweetness of plant nectar made of its full-made. The sale of sugar from sugar as pure natural honey is not only a violation of good faith principles, but also a violation of the law against consumers.

Thus, in order to preserve market order and the health rights of consumers, this sugar-fed sugar, which contains high sugar content, cannot be sold as a good commodity. Good bee-keeping grounds have a high degree of commitment to quality, preferring to phase out earlier mixed honey and not to allow bad sugar to go to market for damage.

After learning about the details behind them, we are more aware of how important it is to select a brand with good reputation and a transparent public examination. It is hoped that this in-depth analysis will help you to create a correct honey shopping concept, avoid the bad quality of the product of the unsatisfied and protect the sweet health of yourselves and their families!
