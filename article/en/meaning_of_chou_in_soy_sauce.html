In order to understand this word, we need to first go back to the traditional place of origin and to make it. In the traditional dialects of the Guangdong and the South, the most central term is "ticking" or "extracting".

It's a "smoking" technique in traditional engineering.

In ancient and traditional sauce gardens, soybeans, soybeans or black beans need to be boiled and made, then placed in giant cays, with long natural sunburns and fermentation in sunlight. Over time, beans and salt water are slowly decomposed by microbes, and fine matter is released into the liquid.

When the first stage of the fermentation is completed, the masters will "take out" the first fermented, best-quality, clear-coloured head juice from the casket, in the form of a squeeze or a funnel, which we call today.

What's raw? Where's birth?

The word "live" is not about raw food, but rather about "fresh" and "precious." Bumping is the fermentation of the head or the front, and its colours are usually clearer in red and brown, and the quality is thinner, the greatest feature of which is that it is heavy and fresh.

What's old? How's old?

The rest of the sauce left in the casket did not end when the smoke was removed. They will remain in the jars, experience longer periods of tan and sun, or go through secondary fermentation and specific craft processing. After a long period of heavy sauce, the color becomes thicker and the quality is thicker.

At this stage of deep processing, the tradition is also to add the appropriate caramel color to the velvet and taste to make it perfect for the red sauce when cooking. Thus, the word "old" is used to represent the staleness of the years, the longer time and the rich quality of life.
