Surprise! You must eat grapefruit for Mid-Autumn! The sound and legend behind your back is immediately understood!

Author: Treasure Date of issue: 13 August 2026 Update: 13 August 2026 Reading time: About 3 minutes
HERO FIGURE

Every mid-Autumn, the other part of the moon cake is a grapefruit! Many will wonder: why do you have to eat grapefruit in mid-Autumn? In fact, the tradition is surrounded by many hymns, a symbol of unity, historical legends and practical health considerations. From the hymns and the hyenas to peace, to the thoughts of the hyenas to their return home, and to the practical function of producing and defusing oil, grapefruit has become indispensable on the Chinese mid-fall table.

Mid-Autumn Festival is August 15th, which coincides with the autumn harvest of grapefruit. In Taiwan, the best food conditions were reached during this period, as were the Qindan, the Mashedu, the West Shoes, and the Chinese mainland. The fruit is full of juicy, sour and sweet, and it fits with high sugary and oily mooncakes and barbecues and becomes the ideal digestive assistant. But it is not enough to talk only about the time order to explain why it is considered a "must eat" holiday fruit.

Say hello to Yuko and Yuko.

One of the most frequently mentioned reasons is the homogeneity of "fruit" and "woo". The ancients believed in peace and blessings for the whole family in the Mid-Autumn and Mid-Autumn Hours, so eating grapefruit was equivalent to Yuko, hoping that the moon would protect the health, family and happiness of the children. The other level of sound is "Traco." Mid-Autumn is a round-table festival, and if the travelers who are far from home can go home, or take care of themselves by eating grapefruit, they will add a warm emotional link to this round-rolling fruit. In some of the local dialects, grapefruits are closer to having sons, and are thus given the blessing of having sons and daughters.

In addition to this, the grapefruit is so cluttered that it responds to the full moon, as a symbol of the "Rally of the Moon". There is also a popular habit of wearing skins of grapefruit on children's heads as a blessing for them to be smart and safe to grow up, and even to say that grapefruit smells to awaken their minds. These layers of hysteria make grapefruits grow from pure time fruit to a blessed holiday symbol.

Taiwan has legends of the killing of grapefruit and the cutting of thieves' heads.

In some parts of Taiwan, especially in the area of Kayin and the cloud forest, the term "killing a grapefruit" or "cutting a thief's head" is spreading. At the beginning of the end of the year, Zhu Won-hyun was ambushed by thieves in the garden of the grapefruit, then ordered to kill the bandits and sacrifice their soldiers with their heads. As the world evolves, it is replaced by grapefruits in the shape of human heads, which are cut off as if they were "killing the head of a thief" and as if they were trying to keep away from evil and guard the house. It's a local legend, but it's an extra mystery and a historical thickening of the grapefruit.

Practical: Decomposition, Nutrition and Health Values

In addition to the cultural connotations, the nutrition and taste of the grapefruit itself have also made it ecstatic in mid-fall. Mooncakes and barbecues tend to be heavy, and sweet, sweet grapefruits can quickly decompress and turn their stomachs back on their mouths. The grapefruit is rich in vitamin C (which is much higher than many commonly seen fruits), diets of nectar, beryllium and natural fruit and glue. The heat is low, it belongs to low GI fruit, it helps digestion, helps control blood sugar, and it benefits the cardiovascular environment. According to the Chinese medical opinion, grapefruit is sorely accommodated that it is very suitable for the fall.

However, attention needs to be drawn to the fact that grapefruit may interact with certain medications (e.g., blood pressure, anti-altamine, etc.) and that it is not appropriate to overdose athopaedic patients. In the selection, it is suggested that a sharper head, a broad tail, a thin skin, and a very heavy, heavy grapefruit is more likely to be bought with thick, juicy, good fruit. Taiwan’s most famous soybeans, often sold empty before mid-fall, is proof of a perfect match between quality and time.

From the festival moon to the modern table.

The tradition of the Mid-Autumn Festival dates back to the Zhou Dynasty, and the atmosphere of the Tang Song period has become more important. The ancients believed in praying for the blessing of the moon to bring the family together, while the grapefruits were born just before mid-fall and after autumn, with many hymns of good and good, which became a ceremonial fruit for worship and food. In modern times, although the rituals are being diluted, the habit of eating grapefruit, wearing grapefruit hats and sharing them with their families is still widespread and becomes an extremely representative part of Chinese middle-fall culture.

Whether it's the blessing of Yuko, the thoughts of Yuko's return, the symbol of a round circle, or simply because it's delicious and obnoxious, it has become the annual ritual of many families for the Mid-Autumn Festival. And when you strip off thick grapefruits and cut off the fruit, you continue a centuries-old cultural conversation that brings the hope of peace, unity and health into the hearts of your family.

Next mid-Autumn, you might want to learn more about the stories behind your back, and more about the grapefruit in your taste. It's not just a fruit, it's a beautiful symbol of blessing, love and season. And every mid-fall will be full of moons and a full room of grapefruit, and peace will be upon them.
