The Distinction between Mother Language User and Total User

To assess the prevalence of a language, linguists usually divide users into two broad categories: the first is a "mother-language user" — a language that has been learned since childhood and is used as the main daily communication language; and the second is a "second language user" — a group of people who use it as a foreign language or as an extra communication tool to learn.

If the population of a single mother tongue (first language) is counted, Chinese Mandarin is steadily ranked at the top of the world with a large population base, with hundreds of millions of faithful mother tongue users, showing strong cultural roots and geographical influence.

World total user winner

However, as we expand our horizons and add "second language users" to the list, the winner of the list is proud in English. Today, the number of total users worldwide is almost 1.5 billion, reflecting the unshakeable and absolute dominance of English in international business, science and technology, scientific research, education and the media.

In addition to English and Chinese, two giants that have surpassed the billion mark, the world's top languages include Hindi and Spanish. Indian has an extremely broad and rich video-enjoying culture in the South Asian continent, while Spanish is extremely powerful in the Americas across many countries in Europe and Latin America.

Other world languages that cannot be ignored

In addition to the above-mentioned top languages, French, Arabic, Bengali, Portuguese and Russian are among the most important players on the modern global list. For example, French is widely used in many African and European countries, while Arabic plays a central and indispensable communication role in North Africa and the Middle East.

Most of these languages have benefited from historical cultural transmission, colonial trajectory, or the rapid convergence of the modern economy and the region. The backs of every language represent a remarkable historical evolution and the culmination of civilization.
