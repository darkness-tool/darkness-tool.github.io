The origin of Father's Day 88 in Taiwan.

According to history, 88 Fathers' Day in Taiwan dates back to the beginning of the 20th century. At that time, as Western culture was gaining ground, Taiwan began to learn to celebrate the festivals, and Father's Day entered the public eye. In 1949, Taiwan officially celebrated Father's Day on 8 August each year, a decision that has continued. As the pronunciation of "eight" is close to "dad", he chose this day to celebrate his father's holiday. Such an arrangement not only makes people feel friendly, but also increases the importance attached to the role of the father. The way Father's Day is celebrated is becoming more and more diverse with time. Many families will choose to go out for dinner on this day or to offer special gifts.
