There was a storm on the outside of the marathon! Will the host cancel it? 90% of the runners fear the truth!

Author: Yuki Date of issue: 23 November 2025 Update: 23 November 2025 Reading time: About 8 minutes

Marathon runners' worst nightmare: The day of the race, the sky rained. Every man who runs a marathon outside his name has watched the weather forecast one night before the match, praying in his heart: "Don't rain." However, the weather in Taiwan has changed dramatically, especially during the fall-winter to spring competitions, often early in the morning or in the middle of the day, with clouds and thunder pourings in the first half of the day. At this point, everyone will ask the same question: "Will it be cancelled?" The answer is not as simple as you think, and the lead agency has a set of strict criteria for judgment. This paper will reveal in its entirety the "rainy weather code" of the country's large marathon, both within and outside the country, so that you can pre-evaluate the risks before you sign up. And I'll teach you how to protect yourself and finish the race if it's a rain war. Whether you're a rookie or an old bird, it's an obligatory pre-game! Keep looking. Don't let the weather ruin your dream!

First of all, there is a myth that rain, rain or even rain will not be lifted by most of the marathons. Major national events such as Taipei Marathon, Tainangudu International Marathon, and Gao Yu Marathon have almost never been cancelled for mere rain. Not to mention, Tokyo, Boston, Berlin, London, and so many of these are recorded in the rain. The organizers usually issue weather alerts one night or the morning before the competition, but rarely call cards directly. Since the cancellation of the competition involved huge costs for tens of thousands of people in terms of transportation, accommodation, sponsorship, and medical resources, unless there was a real "life-threatening" situation, it would be hard to do so. So if you're only in the rain, 99% still has to run. There are only the following extremes. And then we'll tell you the truth. Don't ask me if it's gonna be canceled.

What's going to really cancel? Four red wires once and for all.

1. The Weather Service issues "road alarms" or "typhoon alerts" and the track is within reach. 2. The weather forecast for the day was "high rains above the level" and accompanied by "high-risk thunderstorms" (continuous lightning). 3. The race lanes contain more than 30 cm of water, and there are landslides or the collapse of road trees. 4. The temperature of body temperature is below 5°C and is accompanied by a heavy storm, resulting in a serious risk of temperature loss (very rare). Each of these items is in place, and the host usually suspends or reschedules at 4-6 a.m. In 2023, for example, there was a marathon in the south, which was cancelled directly on the day before the race. But if it's just "The Rain" or "The Rain" doesn't meet the suspension criteria, it's almost normal. And that's why many runners say, "It's not normal to rain in Taiwan's Marathon." When you know these standards, you can judge risks ahead of time, without the insomnia of the night preceding the race. Then I'll show you how to get ready to run fast and safe if it's raining.

A list of equipment for the marathon in the rain.

The worst thing to worry about running in the rain is not getting wet, but a blisters' feet, a cold, a slide. It is recommended to wear: Gore-Tex light waterproof coats (cool and airproof), fast-dry sweatpants, running shoes for water spray treatment. Don't wear new shoes. They must be worn for more than 50 kilometres before they can bubble. Many people put newspapers or waterproof socks in their shoes, with limited test results, and suggested that they should be wet. The point is to change dry socks + dry clothes immediately after the race, so as not to lose heat. In addition, garbage bags have been prepared as raincoats, warm bags, and dry towels have been placed in end-of-the-life bags. The head is covered in sunshields or duck tongue caps to prevent the rain, so that no hair hats can draw water and weigh. The eyes are fogged? A little baby shampoo in the glasses. The water at the supply station must be cold, and it is recommended that the tea or hot drinks wait at the end. The most important thing is to slow down, narrow down, focus on the road, and not push for the PB. It's the best victory in the rain!

Many of the runners share that it is easy to get good results in the rain because it is cold and not hot. As a result, there were more people who broke the PB than in the sky. It's more of a rain war legend abroad, like the 2018 Boston Marathon storm + strong wind, and many legends have emerged. The rain, though gruesome, tends to be the most memorable race in the runaway career. Many cried at the end of the day, saying, "I am so strong," so instead of fearing the rain, it would be better to prepare and change their minds. The weather can't be controlled, but the attitude can. The next time you sign up for the marathon, you see a high rate of rain and no longer have to hesitate. Because you already know that the rain is not going to cancel easily!

Conclusion: 99% of the rain-drive marathons run, and there's no end to being ready!

The outdoor marathon will hardly be cancelled because of the simple rain, unless extreme conditions such as typhoons, thunderstorms, severe water accumulations are reached. Instead of being worried before the race, it's better to prepare waterproofs and adjust the mind. Despite their hard work, running in the rain often becomes the most memorable memory and glory for the runners. Next time I see the rain before the race, I don't have to ask if it'll be canceled. Because the true runner was never afraid of the rain, but afraid of not preparing. May every race of the marathon be completed safely, and the rain be as good as the rain. Now go and check your equipment and prepare for the next battle. We shall meet at the end.
