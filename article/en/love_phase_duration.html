Surprised! What's the time? The truth is unexpected!

Author: Fan Date of issue: 16 June 2025 Update: 16 June 2025 Reading time: about 6 minutes

You think it'll last forever? The truth may surprise you! Lovetime is the best and most passionate period of love. In this period, couples are deeply attracted to each other, with tolerance and illusions. However, how long can this sweet time last? According to studies, the average length of a relationship period is about six months to two years. The high levels of dopamine in the brain during this period are a source of tremendous happiness. But as time goes by, passion becomes more and more stable. Psychologists believe that true love is not just passion, but the accumulation of trust, understanding and commitment. If the passion of the relationship goes smoothly to a steady period, the relationship will last longer. So, what are the factors that affect the duration of the relationship?

According to experts, factors such as individuality, the way in which people live, and the external environment can affect the length of the relationship period. For example, long distance from love may prolong the period of love, as fewer meetings are held and each gathering is valued more. And the couples who meet each day may move faster to a steady date, as the passions turn into daily. In addition, stress, work, and family expectations can affect the speed at which couples' passions recede. Many misunderstandings that the end of the period of love is a sign of a cooling of emotions, which in fact is a shift to a more mature phase. Long-term love requires sustained effort and business, not just a moment of passion. Knowing your relationship patterns helps to better adjust relationships. Have you ever wondered what relationship you're in?

The three stages of love.

Psychological studies show that the period of love is usually divided into three stages. The first stage is a period of passion, lasting between 1 and 6 months, where couples are strongly attracted to each other, ignoring each other's shortcomings. The second stage, which lasts six months to one year, begins to face the challenge of individual differences and reality. The third stage is a steady period of time, usually over a year, and it turns into deep attachment and trust. In a steady period, passion has diminished, but it has become more interconnected. Many of the long-standing relationships continue to develop at this stage. Successful love is not just a passion, it also involves how to manage emotions. It is the communication and understanding between couples that makes relations stronger. If there's enough understanding after a period of love, then it can grow.

How does it last?

Although the period of love cannot last forever, there are ways to prolong it. First, maintaining a sense of freshness is the key, trying new ways of dating, travelling or learning new things together. Second, there are surprises in due course, such as small gifts or a romantic atmosphere. Thirdly, deep communication, sharing of inner thoughts and feelings, and increasing understanding. It is also necessary to respect each other's independence and give each other space. Having a sense of humor is also an important factor in maintaining passion, and a soft smile can keep feelings closer. If couples continue to create good memories, passion will not fade away. Finally, don't forget to strengthen emotional ties, such as a common vision of the future. The essence of love is not just passion, but long-term affection and company.
