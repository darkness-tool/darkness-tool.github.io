Why do mosquitoes always hum in their ears? You'll never know the truth!

Author: Treasure Date of issue: 8 June 2025 Update: 8 June 2025 Reading time: about 5 minutes

Have you ever been awakened by the buzzing in your ears at night? The sound came from the little mosquitoes, who danced in the air and seemed to focus on our ears. But why do mosquitoes prefer to hum in human ears? Scientists think it has to do with mosquitoes' pandering and food-seeking habits. The male mosquitoes make a specific buzzing noise when courting, which attracts the female mosquitoes, which is clearer in the ears. In addition, the sensitivity of mosquitoes to CO2 and body temperature makes them more interested in being near human faces. This is not only for survival, but also for the reproduction of future generations. So when you hear mosquitoes buzzing in your ears, they actually show their charm! But it's not a happy music, it's a irritating music. So, is there any way to get rid of these impassioned guests? And then we're going to explore some of the little anti-septic techniques.

First of all, the most direct way to use mosquito repellents is through a wide range of products available on the market, with natural ingredients. Moreover, keeping the interior clean and eliminating mosquitoes is a fundamental solution. They clean up water regularly to avoid mosquitoes growing near your home. The use of mosquito nets is also an effective defence, especially when sleeping. In addition, certain plants, such as mints and onions, have natural mosquito repellent effects and can be considered for planting on the balcony. Moreover, wearing light clothing reduces the appeal of mosquitoes, which usually prefer black and dark clothes. Finally, maintaining air flow, opening windows or using fans can effectively drive out mosquitoes. These ways not only help you get away from mosquito harassment, but also make your life more comfortable. I hope these little tricks will help you get a better life in the mosquito season!

The spirit and habit of mosquitoes.

The mosquitoes play an important role in the living system, and they are a source of food for many predators. However, mosquitoes have a very strong reproductive capacity, which makes their numbers easily out of control. In some areas, mosquitoes have even become the main vector for the spread of disease, requiring special attention. Therefore, knowledge about mosquitoes helps us control their numbers more effectively. For example, the males of mosquitoes usually do not suck blood, but rely on the juice of plants for their survival. In contrast, female mosquitoes need to draw blood to obtain the protein they need to reproduce. And that's why we often feel that mosquitoes are particularly close to humans. Moreover, mosquitoes tend to peak at dusk and dawn, so we need to be more vigilant during these periods. By understanding the nature of mosquitoes, we can respond more effectively to the challenge of these little creatures.

In addition to the above protective measures, there are some common misunderstandings that need to be clarified. For example, there is some evidence that some people think beer attracts mosquitoes. Alcohol spreads different odours and attracts mosquitoes. In addition, sweets can increase the attractiveness of mosquitoes, which are very sensitive to sugar. So, during the mosquito's active season, proper control over food consumption can have unintended effects. Studies have shown that certain fragrance products, such as perfume or shampoo, can also be a trigger for mosquitoes. Therefore, the selection of some fragrance-free skins may be effective in reducing the exposure of mosquitoes. Anyway, understanding the behaviour and habits of mosquitoes helps us better protect ourselves. As technology advances, there may be more new ways in the future to help us solve this problem.

Conclusion

The mosquitoes are small, but their impact is not small. Understanding the behaviour of mosquitoes helps us not only to avoid bites, but also to promote peace. It is hoped that effective precautions will be taken in the same time as summer. In the days ahead, let us work together to create a safer environment for ourselves and others. Remember, mosquitoes, though annoying, understand their nature and make us more intelligent. May you sleep in peace every summer night, without the noise of mosquitoes!
