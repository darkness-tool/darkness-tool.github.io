Why does the dryer get hot? You'll never know why!

Author: Arrested Date of issue: 4 June 2025 Update: 4 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Do you know why the dryer gets hot? Drying agents are a common feature of our daily lives and are often used to protect food and electrical products from damp. But what many people don't know is, these little dryers. In some cases, heat is generated. Why is that? In this article, we will open the mysterious veil of drying and explore the science behind it! Degenerants are usually water-sorting substances, such as beryllium or chlorinated beryllium, which absorb water in the air. When these matter absorbs moisture, chemical reactions occur, and the process releases heat, so the dryer gets hot. This situation is not only widely applied in industry, but also visible in the home. For example, the use of drying agents in damp environments can be effective in preventing the flow of articles, while also bringing unexpected heat. This heat release process, which is actually the result of a physical variation, allows us to rethink the function of the dryer. When you learn about these scientific principles, you will find that the drying agent is not just a simple damp-proof tool, but a wise little helper! Next time you use the dryer, don't forget the interesting science behind it!

Types and characteristics of drying agents

There is a wide variety of drying agents, the most common of which are beryllium, chlorinated beryllium and activated carbon. Neptunium is a transparent particle that can effectively absorb moisture and can be reused. Perchloride is present in solid form and is extremely humid and suitable for the wet environment. The active carbon is mainly used for the adsorbing of the aroma and damp, and its adsorption capacity is not limited. The work of these dryers is based on their chemical properties, which react to moisture and release heat. During the course of use, these materials absorb the water around them and release thermal energy during the process of adsorption. That's why sometimes we feel the dryer's fever. This heat, though not dangerous, is part of their work. Understanding the characteristics of different drying agents helps us better to choose the right product. The choice of the appropriate drying agent would have a better effect depending on the environment and the demand.

Application scene for drying agent

The application scene for the drying agent is very broad and can be seen from the food package to the wave-proof of the electronic product. In food packages, drying agents are effective in preventing food changes and prolonging the period of protection. In electrical products, anti-tide drying agents can prevent internal components from becoming fluent and affect product performance. In addition, drying agents are often used in clothing storage to avoid deterioration due to damp. The use of drying agents is even more important in special situations, such as the packaging of medical devices. They can ensure that the product remains dry during the transport and storage process to prevent damage. As technology develops, the application of drying agents continues to expand. More and more companies are beginning to realize the value of the drying agent and include it in the design of the product. These little dryers, not only protect the product, but also facilitate our lives. In the future, we can expect drying agents to be used in more areas to make life better.

Safety of the dryer

Safety is an important factor we have to consider when using drying. Most of the drying agents are harmless to humans, but care needs to be taken to avoid misappropriation. Special care is required for some special uses of drying agents, such as beryllium chloride, as it may stimulate skin. In the case of drying, it is recommended to follow the product instructions to avoid direct contact. In addition, the storage of drying agents should be noted and placed in places where children cannot be reached. Understanding the characteristics and methods of use of drying agents can effectively reduce potential risks. Of course, the correct use can also help to prolong the life of the dryer.
