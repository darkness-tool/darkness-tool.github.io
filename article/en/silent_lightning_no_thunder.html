It's amazing! The night sky has been lightning and no thunder? The truth is amazing!

Author: Yuki Date of issue: 14 January 2026 Update: 14 January 2026 Reading time: about 5 minutes

This is definitely a creepy night view! On a hot summer night, did you ever look up and see a white or purple light shining in the sky as if the whole cloud was glowing, but you couldn't hear half the thunder? This phenomenon of "blasting lightning without thunder" has shocked many people, even those who believe it to be an alien signal or a sign of the end of the world! In fact, it's a common but mysterious "silent lightning" in nature, hidden behind our backs in amazing science, so let's unmask the veil!

This phenomenon, often referred to scientifically as the "hot lightning" or "silent thunder", is not really the "no thunder," but the thunder has been scattered before it reached your ears. Lightning and thunder always occur at the same time, but the speed of light is much faster than the sound (about 300,000 kilometres per second and 340 metres per second), so we always see the flash before we hear it. But when lightning happens in a very distant place, the thunder cannot reach us at all, creating this wonderful picture of the "lightless lightning."

That's why it's so far away!

According to weather experts, thunder can only spread in the air about 10 to 15 kilometres away, beyond which the sound disappears as air frictions and declines. On the contrary, the light of lightning can be reflected, reflected and transmitted hundreds of kilometres away. Especially at night, when there is no sunlight interference, and when the clouds are thin, the light is more visible from far away, which is why it is so often seen in the summer that the lightning is silent.

In addition to the distance factor, the other common cause is the `reflection upward' of sound waves. When the temperature on the ground is lower at night and there is a warm atmosphere above it, the sound waves will be bent up like a ray of light, so that we do not receive any thunder on the ground. This is particularly common in Taiwan during the summer, where the ferocious truncated clouds are often formed in the open sea, 10 kilometres from the island, and where people are able to see clearly the clouds in the clouds (inside the clouds being charged), without hearing any sound, like a silent light show.

People are witnessing the footage and the excitement.

Many people in Taiwan have shared their experiences on Facebook and on Thareads. It's like, "Thainan night sky flashes once every two seconds, for more than half an hour, but it's so quiet!" After these films went crazy, weather experts came to the fore, explaining that this was actually a thunderstorm far away from the sea, that a booming current had a very high rate of lightning transmission, and that, together with high-temperature water in the summer, had perfect silent lightning conditions. Internet users call it science, not magic!

It's worth mentioning that this is a beautiful situation, but don't take it lightly! Even if the thunder cannot be heard, the sight of lightning means that there is a thunderstorm in the vicinity or approaching, and there is still a risk of lightning. Experts remind us that thunderstorms should avoid outdoor activity and stay away from high objects and water to be truly safe.

It's nature's amazing light show.

The silent lightning is not only a scientific reality, but also a beautiful vision feast of nature for humanity. And when you next see the sky shimmering on the night of the summer, then take a quiet heart and appreciate the shock of this quiet, and remember that behind it is the race of light and sound, and the wonderful illusion of distance.
