It's the old rules of the farm! How dare you disobey the traditional taboos? The elders will be angry!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 24 January 2026 Update: 24 January 2026 Reading time: About 4 minutes

The old rules must not be forgotten! Every year on the New Year of the Farmer's Day, family members display lights, springs, firecrackers. But behind this glamorous back, the old people always remember what they cannot do. These old rules were not intended to frighten people, but rather the wisdom of their ancestors for thousands of years, hoping for a new year of good weather, peace for their families and prosperity. If you don't take care of yourself, the elders may shake their heads and say, "Hey, be careful this year!" Let's elaborate on the most common rules and taboos of the New Year of Agriculture and give you a happy New Year!

The old rules of the preparatory phase.

The year began in the month of the year! At the beginning of the month, we ate eight porridges of gruelling and sweetness, and on the twenty-third or twenty-fourth of the month, the Lord of the Sunshine gave a gift for the good and evil of the family and prayed for the next year. This was followed by a major sweep called "Screeching the old fabric" to clear all the dust and dirt from the house and to wipe out the bad air and welcome the new fate. But remember, you have to sweep through the door. You can't sweep outside, before you sweep the money out! Others have seen old rules.

To wear red clothes and red accessories for good luck; to eat oranges and apples for good; to send firecrackers for evil; and to pay tribute to the ancestors and the land. Debts are to be paid out years ago, not years later; no funerals, no visits, no bad words. These rules, which have superstition elements, show that Chinese people value their families, pursue their blessing and continue to embrace a new culture.
