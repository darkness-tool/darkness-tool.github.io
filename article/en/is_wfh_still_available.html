Does Hong Kong have a WPH? Worker's stupid!

Author: Jijio Date of issue: 13 June 2025 Update: 13 June 2025 Reading time: about 6 minutes

It's your biggest concern! After almost three years of anti-prevalence, is the long past or is it going to be? Many people can't find the word "WFH" in the dictionary, but when they think about the freedom to work in their homes, their hearts are full of shit. We can't help but ask, "Do we have any WFP now?" The government has fully restored its face-to-face services, and businesses continue to call back their employees. However, most of the workers ' voices are that they still expect more liveliness and choice. According to the data, some businesses revert to the traditional business model, but a number of positions remain in the household. Who works at home and who is forced to return to the desk? Let's explore the current and future of the current WFP.

According to the needs of different business cultures and industries, the implementation of WFP has varied considerably. Science and technology, new and free professions are often able to continue to work far away without office space restrictions. On the contrary, traditional ministries of finance, manufacturing, catering and so on have required employees to return to their offices. Many companies have adopted mixed business models (Hybrid work model) that provide for a specific number of days of work in a week. Even with the WFP policy, employees complain about inefficient communication and home interference. Although many companies offer remote tools, VPNs and cloud services, their usage varies from one company to another. The ongoing debate on WFP has made it necessary for employees and supervisors to re-adapt and coordinate. The next step is to explore the real face of WFP from both the corporate and the employee perspectives.

The WFP Situation

The Hong Kong Government has gradually restored public services since April 2022 and plans to fully restore normal operations as of April 21st. Following the Government's recall, many private companies have also abolished long-standing home-based (WFH) measures or converted them into hybrid models. According to news reports, most of the large companies demand that workers return to face-to-face work to improve team cohesion and efficiency. There are a number of critics who believe that this may reduce the welfare and productivity of employees. On the other hand, it has also been pointed out by companies that office collaboration can promote creative ideas and instant communication. Despite this, some companies retain home-based working days, which allow employees to freely choose their workplace. As a result, the whole flow of WFH in Hong Kong has shifted from a full remote to a "selective remote" phase. What kind of company are you in?

Business surveys.

According to the latest survey, most companies are turning to "Mixed Office 2.0", which combines remote and physical elements. This model, while providing a lively one, brings with it higher management challenges and communication costs. Many human resource managers have pointed out that maintaining team culture and monitoring results is the most difficult part. Some companies, such as Tesla, Meta and others, have requested full staff to be stationed in their offices or to return in shifts. At the same time, there are new and innovative companies that are committed to WFP and that emphasize results orientation rather than performance. Experts remind companies to tailor WFP policies to the nature of their work and the needs of their employees. In the future, an effective balance between trust and results will determine whether the hybrid model is successful or not. Managers and employees must explore suitable work arrangements in order to win the game.

The voice of the employee and the challenge.

Many employees believe that WFP has improved quality of life and saved time and transportation costs. However, there are also complaints that it is difficult to interact effectively with managers and colleagues when working at home. According to studies, WFH, WAH (Work at Home) and WFA (Work from Anywhere) have different definitions and applications. Workers using the World at Home are confined to their residences and maintain a higher concentration. Work from Anywhere allows business to take place in any location, suitable for several pastoralists and liberals. In addition, remote work raises mental health and border management issues, requiring staff tools for self-regulation and time management. Businesses and employees need to educate and communicate to build the best work in the remote. Only clear rules and support can make WFP work.

Future trends

In the future, mixed business will become the mainstream, and flexible hours and site selection will be further enhanced. Companies need to invest in cloud-based cooperation platforms, security certification and staff training to respond to the demand for remote jobs. At the same time, managers must learn new ways of leading, focusing on work results rather than working time. They also need to improve self-regulation and digital communication skills to ensure work-life balance. The government and the Association can promote remote business guidelines to help the parties develop sound policies. The future type of work will not be a binary choice, but a combination of pluralism, resilience and human nature. It is only by keeping up with the times that the sense of competition and happiness can be maintained in rapid change. Now, start working with the organization to make the best model for your business!
