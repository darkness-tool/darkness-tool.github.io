What does iPhone mean by green, yellow, orange, red? Lightning's hidden machine. All eyes are stupid!

Author: Tiger. Issued: 1 January 2026 Update: 1 January 2026 Reading time: about 6 minutes

It's a hidden function that surprises countless iPhone users! Have you ever discovered in your daily use of iPhone that the battery icons of the status bar have become green, yellow, orange or red? There's even a lightning sign that gives you the fog and wonders if there's a problem with your phone? Actually, this is Apple's well-designed battery state alert system, which uses different colours and symbols to tell you how much power you have on your phone, how it is charged, and how it can be warned. When many users first saw these changes, they thought it was incredible, even the system bug! Today, we're going to unmask these colors of mystical veils so that you can understand the true meaning of the iPhone image and avoid being surprised again. These little details will not only be practical, but will also help you to better manage the life of the battery and extend the use of mobile phones.

First of all, the most common change is that battery icons change from normal white or black to other colours. It's all about your power, your charging state and your system mode. From the beginning of the iOS system, Apple introduced these visual tips so that users can see the battery at once. You know, whether you're new or old, knowing what these colors mean makes you more comfortable with the iPhone. Especially in times of electrical anxiety, these tips help you to charge or adjust your habits. Many netizens shared that when the battery turns red, they panic and search for a charger; when they see yellow, they find a mode of power-saving. These changes, though simple, contain Apple's careful consideration of the user experience, so let's explore them step by step.

What does a green battery mean?

Green battery icons are the most reassuring color! When your iPhone is charging, no matter how much it is, the battery icon becomes green, accompanied by a lightning symbol. This means that the cell phone is absorbing power normally, and the charge is running smoothly. Green is a "charging" device used by Apple to divide charging and non-charging states. Even if the power is low, if you plug in the charger, you'll see a green image, so you know that the phone is already refilling the energy. Many users say that when they see a green battery, they relax because it means that the phone does not suddenly turn off. At the same time, in the latest version of iOS, green may also appear in battery graphs, which represent normal charging processes. If you see a green flash sign, it's a clear charge confirmation signal.

The secret of yellow batteries and low power patterns.

When the battery is yellow, many people start to freak out and think the phone's broken! In fact, this is a sign opened by the iPhone Low Power Mode. When power is less than 20%, the system automatically recommends that this mode be activated to reduce background activity, reduce brightness and disable some of the special effects. If you open or accept advice manually, the battery turns yellow. Even when charge is being made, yellow will continue to be displayed until mode shut down (usually more than 80% automatic). The yellow battery reminds you that your mobile phone is in a state of power loss and that some of its features, such as automatic downloading, and the suspension of Hey Siri, are available. The design allows users to know why their phones are slowing down and avoid misunderstandings. A number of netizens shared that yellow batteries saved their power when they left, keeping their phones up for hours. Do you want to turn it off? To set the > battery can easily switch.

Orange and Red Warnings

Orange batteries are less common and appear mainly in the graph of battery usage (set > battery) after iOS 18, while orange strips or lightning indicate slow chargers (lower than 20W) and lower charge rates. The system uses orange to remind you to switch to a higher power charger, to fill the power faster and to make the battery healthy. Red batteries are a serious warning. When power is less than 20% and low power mode is open, the icons turn red, reminding you to charge the battery immediately, or the cell phone may be shut down soon. Even if the charge is very low, the red may be displayed first. Red is a crisis signal. Let the user move. These color changes make iPhone more intelligent and help us avoid the embarrassment of running out of electricity.

When you learn the color of these batteries, you'll find the iPhone is really sweet! Green ecstasy charger, yellow smart saver, orange velocities, red emergency alerts, plus the flash sign clearly marked charger. These little designs are not only practical, but also enable you to manage the battery's health better and avoid excessive power consumption or damage.
