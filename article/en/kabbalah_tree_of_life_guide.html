Unearthing the mystery of the universe and the mind: deeply deconstructing the ten key points and structures of the tree of Karbala.

Author: Tiger. Date of issue: 8 September 2026 Update: 8 September 2026 Reading time: About 8 minutes

This is a mysterious map of the creation of a map of the universe and of the human mind. In Western mythology and Jewish philosophy tradition of "Kabala", the Tree of Liberty plays a very central role. It is not just a simple geometric map, but it is considered to be the infinite existence of Ein Sof that pictifies its energy and creates a complete reality model of the entire mass universe. Through an in-depth study of this tree, the mythologists and practitioners have tried to solve the ultimate mystery of the origins of the universe and the location of human beings therein.

The core structure of the Kabala tree is made up of ten "Sephiroth" (the single number is Sephirah) and 22 paths to connect to them. These ten qualities are organized in three vertical pillars, each of which represents the pillar of compassion on the right, the pillar of hardness on the left, and the pillar of balance in the middle. This structure perfects the dynamical balance between the expansion and contraction of all things in the universe, their initiative and their being moved.

The Holy Threes and Ten Questions

The top of the tree of life is Kether, the prime point for pure consciousness and the origin of the universe, which is the product of unlimited light constriction into a strange spot. After that, Chokmah, at the top of the right, is a symbol of dynamic creative energy and primitive inspiration, while Binah, at the top of the left, plays the role of a great mother, who is given shape and structure, with the potential to transform abstract energy into concrete. The first three elements together form the sacred triangle of the highest consciousness.

Extending downwards, the key points in the middle are Chesed, Gebrah, which represents power and discipline, and Tiphareth, which lies at the heart of the whole tree, at the heart of beauty and reconciliation. Tiphareth is the heart belt of all energy exchange and balance, perfectly reconciling the extreme expansion of the right side and the excessive limits of the left side, and is an important milestone in the growth of the mind and its self-integration.

From the spiritual to the material flow.

As energy continues to flow downward, it enters the lower half of the point that is closer to the real world. Netzach, on the right, represents lasting motivation, emotional and natural creativity, while on the left, it is Hod, a structure of reason, logic and form. They combine emotions with reason, and they converge down to the ninth key "Yesod."

Yesod, as the foundation, is inspired by the bridge between inside and outside of the world of mind. In the end, all the sacred energy and the flow of the universe condensed at the bottom of the tenth key, the "Malkuth". Malkuth, which represents the physical world and nature in which we live, does not add to the light itself, but receives the total energy from all the above, so that everything can really be seen in reality.

The Hidden Eleven Quality: Da'at

In addition to the 10 key points traditionally identified, there is a very special and mysterious presence in the Kabala tree, which is "Da'at" below the crown and on the middle axis. Da'at is not usually painted as an independent common denominator, because it is the invisible convergence of the first three high-level points when they reach perfect integration. It symbolizes the deep awakening of consciousness and the sacrilegious integration of consciousness, and represents the ultimate wisdom beyond the two-fold rivalry.

Modern thought and revelation of the tree of life.

The Kabala tree is not just a Kosmology model to explain the creation of the universe, but a set of profound spiritual exercises. Kabala scholars believe that the human soul, after having lost its way to the material world, can overcome its demons, cleanse itself step by step along the path of the tree of life, and finally return from the lowest material kingdom to the crown of the source, Kether, to the end of the world.

This unique philosophic system has not only profoundly influenced Western mysticals, Tarot and recent psychology, but has also been widely cited in many contemporary literature, animation and games. By understanding the structure and content of the Kabala tree, we have been able to re-examine the balance of the mind in our troubled modern life and find our own direction on the path to the truth of the universe. The enormity of the tree of Kabala, which has spanned centuries of history, continues to shine in a fascinating light of wisdom. Whether you're the first lover of mystics or the scholar of deep philosophies, this mysterious tree of life and the universe will bring you endless inspiration and thought. Let us all look forward to unmasking, in the next exploration, more ambitious blueprints hidden behind the back of the universe.
