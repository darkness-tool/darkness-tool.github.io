Discover the secrets of mythology!

This is a dark field that challenges the limits of human intelligence and the taboos of mystical science! In the tradition of mythology and Judaism, when people move their eyes away from the luminous tree of divine and light, the shadow behind them will appear. This is the tree of anti-Kabala, also known as the tree of evil or the tree of the shell, that fascinated the mythists and the authors of the phantom.

The name of the tree against Kabala is The Qlipphoth or Qlippoth, which originates in the form of a complex in Hebrew and is intended to be "shell", "skin" or "improper mental energy". It is in complete opposition to the proper Sefirot, which is a symbol of the imbalances, degradation, chaos and dark forces that are marginalized by the divine light in the universe.

History source and structure resolution

Unlike the ancient orthodoxy of Kabala, the modern whole system of the trees of Kabala is relatively new, and it has only been systematized by mystical researchers in the late days. Its structure is similar to that of the tree of life, but it is upside down, made up of ten evils and dark points, from the bottom of the symbolism to the high kingdom to the top, representing rebellion and vanity.

In every one of them is a seal on evil, a negative feeling, and a mighty devil who rules over it. These elements are not mere fictions, but are perceived as the spiritual semblance of the original sins of greed, anger, jealousy, etc., deep inside the human heart, representing the endless depths into which the soul may fall in pursuit of power.

The mystery of popular culture.

As a result of its unique and mysterious structure, the anti-Kabala tree has often been at the heart of well-known ACG and phantom literature. This ancient mystical concept has been given a new life, from the magic of the magic giant tree in the game, to the reactionary and powerful rivalry of the role.

For example, in many Japanese animations and games, the ability of the role to awaken or the ultimate crisis in the world is often linked to the unsealing of the key points against Kabala. With this ancient symbolism, the author adds to his work a deep philosophical and divinely sad color.

Through our exploration of the trees that rebelled against Kabala, we were able to look at the reflections in human history on the eternal arguments between "light and shadow" and "good and evil". This tree of darkness is not only an important subject of mystical research, but also a mirror reflecting the fragile nature of humanity that aspires to transcend boundaries and is easily swallowed up.
