The year of the 28th will win! The house has been wiped out and bad luck has gone!

Author: Ah Hau Date of issue: 11 February 2026 Update: 11 February 2026 Reading time: About 8 minutes

Dirt and bad luck! On December 28th, the family was busy cleaning up their homes, not only to clean up their homes, but also as an important ritual in traditional traditions to remove old, new and evil. After a complete clean-up of the "28" sound, the new year was a year of good fortune and good fortune. You want a new home, a good light? I'll do it one step at a time.

In the '88s, people believed in filth. It brings bad luck and disease, and the gods don't walk into a house of chaos. So, years ago, we cleaned up and swept away the bad news of the past year and welcomed the new spring. This custom is particularly prevalent in the Guangdong region, where families in Hong Kong, Taiwan, and South China are close to home. At the same time, the day is also marked by the abandonment of old habits and new beginnings, as well as the strengthening of family cohesion and the most meaningful way to work together.

We must know the taboos and the preparations before the big sweep.

Traditionally, cleaning begins from the inside, from the top to the bottom of the room, and sweeps the dust out to the door, signalling the bad weather. I'm not sure if I'm going to be able to do that. Do not wash the floor directly from the door, and the garbage is concentrated and then dumped, avoiding financial outflows. It is not appropriate to clean up again in the 30th to the 4th year in order to avoid the loss of wealth. If you miss it, you can clean it up in the first five years. The preparation tools include rags, seaweeds, toothbrushes, sodas, lemons, white vinegar, bleaching water, grapefruit and dust cleaners. Remember to wear gloves to protect your hands.

The most important thing is to take a break! Check the closets, the bookstores, the bed floors, the kitchen cabinets, and discard or donate all the old clothes that have not been used for a year, the food that has expired, the broken power, the damaged items. After the breakup, the space became larger and cleaner. Ask yourself: When was the last time this item was used? Is there any real value? If the answer is no, let it go. New Year's new weather, starting with a decrease.

It's a clean house.

First, from the top: the ceiling, the chandelier, the air cooler, the curtain track and the dust from the cupboard are cleared with a chicken-scaved or vacuum cleaner. It's not like we're going to have to do it again. The walls, doors, windows, furniture surfaces were then wiped. It's the end of the mud. Special attention is paid to window and door glass, which can be wiped in newspapers or micropaste with no trace effect. The windows are sewn with a toothbrush and a cleaning agent. The floor is then drained and then dragged to the ground, and the last towed with the tea leaves, and it is meant to drive away the evil.

Kitchen disaster area: oil star

Kitchen oil is the worst. Heated water with small sodas to make it paste, paint the stove, brush it with toothbrush and flush it. The smoker was removed from the oil net and immersed for 20 minutes with hot water and little soda, and the oil was released. Water taps and tatters with a little sodium, and half a lemonade, and the water is removed. A few minutes of lemonade heating in the microwave, steam softening the residue and cleaning it with a rag. In and out of the ark, there was emptying of food for extended periods and wiping it out with little soda.

The bathroom's anti-fouling.

Bathroom tile sewn sewn with bleach immersed in the kitchen towels for one night and then washed. The toilet was poured into little Soda and jealous, and then it was cleaned. Water taps and flowers are soaked with white vinegar. The tubs and mirrors are polished with little soda and toothpaste, and the light is fresh. The water drains with little soda and hot water. In the end, they sprayed the corner with fresh grapefruit.

And the grapefruit is the 28th Episode. Cut the grapefruit and boil it with water, and add little salt, and then cool it with furniture, and drag or cool it. It is known to drive away evil, prevent disease, and transfer. The modern scientific fingernails of grapefruit contain ketone, which helps to relax. When the house was completely cleaned, it was washed from the inside and out with fresh grapefruit, which was a sign of clean air and of a positive energy.

New sheets, curtains, new beginnings. In the end, the whole family was bathed in the tea and washed for a year of wear and tear.

The year 28 is not just a clean house, but a renewal of the body and soul. Follow the above-mentioned plan, and the home will be clear and bright, and the new year will be a happy year, and the wealth will be great! And may you share your clean-up and welcome your new years!
