Fancy bud! Learn this coconut-juice cake to make you fall in love!

Author: Editor-in-Chief Date of issue: 2 June 2025 Update: 2 June 2025 Reading time: About 3 minutes

Coconut-juice, not only delicious, but also a reminiscent traditional dessert! At annual festivals, coconut-juice cakes are essential for food. The sweet is not only soft, but also fragrances of radiant coconuts, which are reminiscent. Today, we will share an easy-to-teach coconut-juice recipe so that you can make this delicious snack at home!

First, we need to prepare some basic materials, including rice powder, coconuts, sugar and water. The proportion of these materials will directly affect the taste of pastry, and it is recommended that you be prepared as required by the recipe.

Material requirements

Here are some of the materials needed to make coconut juice cakes: - 250 grams of corn. - 200 ml of coconut. - 100 grams of sugar. - 200 ml of water. - A little salt. When we're ready to do this, we can do it!

Make steps

1. Combining rice powder, sugar and salt and then slowly adding water and mixing it evenly until there is no grain. 2. Add coconuts and continue to mix to ensure that all materials are fully integrated. This step is the key to making sure that the pasta is soft!

3. To pour the mixed paste into the pre-prepared voltage, with a light vibration to vent the air. 4. Covered with a pot, the steamer was placed in the steamer and evaporated in the middle fire for about 30 minutes.

After fertilizing, the cake cools a little, and then it is carefully deformed. 6. Slice in small pieces, with fresh coconuts or fruits, which is more fragrance!

Small tricks.

In order to make coconut-juice cake more fragrance, some vanilla may be added to the rice powder or replaced with different flavors of coconut, which can create different layers of taste and aroma.

Time

Coconut-juice cakes are not only suitable for festivals, but also an excellent choice for afternoon tea snacks. A cup of hot tea to please you.

Summary

It's easy to understand how this coconut cake is made, whether it's new or experienced cooks. Try this recipe and surprise your family and friends with your cooking!
