Why is there a powerful dog everywhere and there's no cat? The truth makes people laugh!

Author: Arrested Date of issue: 18 September 2026 Update: 18 September 2026 Reading time: About 9 minutes

Every time we see a powerful dog in the news or in a video, we can't help but admire their professionalism and loyalty. However, have you ever thought about an interesting question: why is a human best friend who is as smart as a cat, besides a dog, and never heard of a profession like "the cat?" Are the cats refusing to work?

To understand this, we have to start with the evolution of animals and their basic nature. Dogs and cats walk out of a completely different path in the course of thousands of years of human fertilization, which also determines their fate in the workplace.

The difference between a natural animal and a proud master.

Dogs are a typical group of animals that need to rely on a team to survive in a wild environment. This nature gives them a high level of compliance instinct for "leaders" or "commanders". When the police give the order, the dogs will see it as being carried out as a group.

In contrast, cats are lone hunters. They are used to being alone in the evolution process, have great control over their territory and have no need to follow the orders of other animals. In the world of cats, they think they're the center of the universe, and humans are just high-ranking poopers.

Why can't cats be trained?

A lot of people think cats are smart, so why can't they sit down, wait, jump like dogs and do discipline training? Actually, it's about incentives and motivation. Dogs are willing to work very hard for training to get their master's praise or a piece of meat.

However, the attitude of cats towards food and praise is very free. Even if you took out the top mashed, if the owl was in a bad mood today, or if he thought the order was boring, he would turn his head and leave behind an awkward trainer. This unforeseeable randomness is a fatal drawback in discipline and efficiency policing.

The size and strength of the mission

Apart from inappropriate personality, body size and physical ability are also a major constraint. Dogs usually need to be equipped with sufficient body and explosive force to respond to dangerous suspects, track them for long periods of time, and even control the perpetrators when necessary. Most cats are small in size, and if they actually go to war against suspects, their safety will be difficult to secure.

Although cats are equally sensitive to their senses of smell and hearing and are suitable to catch rats, they work for hours in high-pressure environments, almost impossible, like drug dogs or search dogs. They may be working half-time to start hairdressing, or they can go straight to a comfortable place to sleep.

There are no cats, but they have more important jobs.

Despite this, there are very few special cases in different parts of the world, such as some police stations that include stray cats as "heads" or "assurance ambassadors". Although they are not in the front line to catch the bad guys, there is an indelible and significant contribution to easing the pressure of police colleagues and to healing the tension.

In conclusion, police dogs can be heroes because they are born happy to cooperate and obey; cats are their most charming place to remain proud and independent. The business is a professional one, and dogs are for the public service, while cats are for the lovely emperor who heads everything at home.

The next time you see a police dog, look at the owner of the cat, and thank them for choosing to live in peace, so that you don't have to be a "worker."
