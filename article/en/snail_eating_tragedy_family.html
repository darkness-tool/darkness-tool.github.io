This is a heartbreaking and shocking real food tragedy! In the eyes of many, life in nature may be filled with the potential for progress, yet some wrong attempts can cost the lives of the entire family. Looking back at the social news of Taiwan's past, a blood-killing case motivated by greed to provide for a living, remains a shadow in the hearts of millions of people.

The event featured a well-known family of established companies in the country. At the time, the second generation of family leaders had listened to the popular bias, mistaking the idea that the African snails, either raw or uncooked, had a very high nutritional value and were able to strengthen their bodies and extend their lives. And not only did they have no protection against such parables, but they nourished them in their homes and brought them to the table.

Deadly delicious: the deadly crisis hidden behind the snails.

African snails do not exist in their own right, but they are the best hosts for many horror parasites and pathogens. When humans eat uncooked snails, the parasites in the body start to hunt without mercy. One of the most notorious killers is Guangdong's blood worm.

After Guangdong's bloodline worms enter the human body, they cannot be properly metabolismed, but will travel with their blood and eventually enter the central nervous system, brain and spinal cord of the human body. The infection starts with severe headaches, fever, nausea, vomiting, neck rigidity, etc., and causes paralysis, coma and even death.

A happy family that collapses in a moment.

During that season of fate, several members of the family, soon after eating the snails, began to suffer severe headaches and neurosis. Initially, people thought it was just a common cold or a physical infirmity, but the situation turned so fast that it was too late to go back.

The sudden parasite infection took the precious lives of many members of the family, and only a few members who were not in the country were spared. A family that was already rich and well-known, has been shattered by a wrong load of food, and has even been facing an imminent catastrophe.

Remember: Don't try.

Once again, this tragic historical event is a bloody warning to the world that wild animals will not be blindly fed. Whether it's snails, snails or all kinds of wild screws, they have to be cooked with a high temperature to eat. It is only by cherishing life and living away from myths that similar tragedies can be avoided.
